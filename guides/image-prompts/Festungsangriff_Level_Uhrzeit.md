## Prompt

```text
Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: square German alliance-announcement graphic for a game community website and in-game alliance post
Primary request: Create an original premium announcement graphic for a coordinated alliance attack on a fortress.
Input images: Image 1 is a style and layout reference only; create a new scene and do not edit or duplicate its central city.
Scene/backdrop: a dark medieval-fantasy strategy hall with navy banners, candlelight and a large tactical map table. On the map stands one massive isolated border fortress with thick walls, four defensive towers, a raised gate and rocky terrain; clearly a fortress, not a populated city.
Style/medium: polished cinematic fantasy infographic, sophisticated dark navy and antique gold, structured and highly legible, informative rather than narrative.
Composition/framing: square poster. Large headline at top; central fortress model on the strategy map; one prominent information plaque; three evenly separated action panels with simple gold pictograms; concise footer. No people or characters as the main motif.
Lighting/mood: warm candlelight, focused, coordinated, official alliance briefing.
Text (render verbatim, exactly once each):
"FESTUNGSANGRIFF"
"FESTUNGSLEVEL & UHRZEIT"
"SIEHE MITTEILUNG"
"ZIELMARKIERUNG PRÜFEN"
"AUF FREIGABE WARTEN"
"GEMEINSAM ANGREIFEN"
"ZUSAMMEN NEHMEN WIR DIE FESTUNG"
Typography: large elegant gold serif headline; clean highly readable German text in gold or warm ivory; no tiny body text.
Constraints: exact German spelling and umlauts; no other text; no people; no logos; no watermarks; no modern sci-fi interface; no recognizable game UI or copyrighted symbols; no apocalyptic red-black look; balanced gold ornamental frames; readable at small size.
```

Die Grafik bleibt absichtlich ohne feste Uhrzeit und ohne festes Festungslevel. Beide Angaben werden für jeden Angriff über die Eingabemaske der Website in die kopierbare Allianz-Mitteilung eingesetzt.
