# Gallery

This section contains the visual collections displayed in the website’s **Gallery**.

- [Avatars](../Avatare/en/README.md) – finished profile and avatar images
- [Character models](../Charaktermodelle/en/README.md) – required references for recurring characters
- [Chat images](../Chatbilder/en/README.md) – scenic images, memes and short stories
- [Reaction images](../Reaktionsbilder/en/README.md) – short, reusable replies and greetings

The gallery folder determines the intended use. The independent visual style S1, S2, S3 or S4 is maintained jointly for chat and reaction images in [`STILINDEX.md`](../../../Galerie/STILINDEX.md).

Additional graphics from `Drachenwissen` and `Styleguides` may also appear in the web gallery, but remain stored with their subject content in the repository.
