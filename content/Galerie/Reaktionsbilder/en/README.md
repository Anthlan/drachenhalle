# Reaction Images

This folder contains short, reusable replies and greetings for alliance chat, such as “Works”, “Great”, “Sorry, What?”, “Morning” or “Good Night”. Their message should be immediately understandable without an ongoing scene.

Scenic or narrative images belong in [`Galerie/Chatbilder`](../../Chatbilder/en/README.md), even when created in chibi style.

## Style Assignment

“Reaction image” describes intended use and does not determine visual style. Reaction images may use S1, S2, S3 or S4. Their required assignments are recorded alongside chat images in the central [`Galerie/STILINDEX.md`](../../../../Galerie/STILINDEX.md).

## Naming Rules

- Pattern: `YYYY_MM_DD_HHMM_Kurztitel_[Hauptfiguren].Dateiendung`.
- Example: `2026_09_25_2331_Passt_Anthlan.png`.
- The timestamp comes first.
- The short title concisely names the reusable reaction or greeting without spaces.
- Clearly identified main characters are appended with underscores.
- Player names preserve confirmed capitalisation; file extensions are lowercase.
- `README.md` is exempt from this image pattern.
