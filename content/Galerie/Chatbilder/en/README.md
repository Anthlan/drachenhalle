# Chat Images

This folder collects scenic or narrative images from alliance life: snapshots, memes, running jokes and short stories. Intended use determines storage. Even an image in chibi style remains a chat image when it tells a scene or action.

Short, reusable replies and greetings belong instead in [`Galerie/Reaktionsbilder`](../../Reaktionsbilder/en/README.md).

## Style Assignment

Chat images can use one of four visual styles independently of their purpose:

- **S1 – Cinematic Alliance Fantasy:** cohesive, semi-realistic scenes with narrative composition
- **S2 – Fantasy Comic & Infographic:** text-, panel- or UI-focused designs in the shared DIE look
- **S3 – Chibi Fantasy:** highly simplified, cute and deliberately exaggerated designs
- **S4 – Z-Routes Post-apocalypse:** bold, grainy survival posters with black-red-white typography, handwritten jokes and clear “Z Routes Redemption” branding

The required assignment of all chat and reaction images is in the central [`Galerie/STILINDEX.md`](../../../../Galerie/STILINDEX.md). Style is inferred neither from the folder nor the filename. New or renamed images without exactly one valid style entry deliberately cause the website build to fail.

## Naming Rules

- Pattern: `YYYY_MM_DD_HHMM_Kurztitel_[Hauptfiguren].Dateiendung`.
- Example: `2026_09_17_2043_KaffeeOderSomea_Anthlan_Somea.png`.
- The timestamp comes first. The short title and main character names contain no spaces; multiple clearly identified main characters are appended with underscores.
- Animals, objects and unnamed supporting characters belong in the short title where needed and are not appended as player names.
- Player names preserve confirmed capitalisation. File extensions are lowercase.
- `README.md` is exempt from this image pattern. Images are stored directly in this folder; style-specific subfolders are not used.
