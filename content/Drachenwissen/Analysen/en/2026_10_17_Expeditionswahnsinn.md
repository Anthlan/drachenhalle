# Observations on Capital Conquest on 17 October 2026

This analysis includes 655 players with at least 1,000 system-recorded points. It describes ranking entries and point totals, not actual contributions to the conquest.

## Recorded point totals

| Metric | S105 | S107 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Players with at least 1,000 points | 271 | 379 |
| System-recorded points | 182,614,549 | 481,105,141 |

S105 records 182,614,549 points and S107 records 481,105,141 points. Five qualifying players from other servers are listed separately. This point comparison does not assess conquest success.

## Observation compared with 10 October

Using the same 1,000-point threshold, S105 is 1.3% below the previous week: 184,959,827 points then versus 182,614,549 now. Its total is therefore approximately unchanged. The opponent changed from S97 to S107; different screenshot times do not allow a standardised performance comparison.

## Effect of the minimum threshold

| Minimum points | S105 · 10 Oct | S105 · 17 Oct |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 500 | 256 | 292 |
| 1,000 | 248 | 271 |
| 5,000 | 234 | 226 |
| 10,000 | 233 | 209 |

The direction of the change in counted players depends on the threshold: it rises at 1,000 points and falls at 5,000 and 10,000. This cannot establish a general increase or decrease in actual participation.

## Our alliance DIE

| Metric | 10.10.2026 | 17.10.2026 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| System-recorded points | 9,563,043 | 5,243,118 |
| Players with at least 1,000 points | 12 | 14 |
| Membership snapshot | 58 | 68 |
| Leaderboard coverage | 20.7% | 20.6% |

At a minimum of 1,000 points, DIE records 5,243,118 rather than 9,563,043 points, a decline of 45.2%. The counted players increase from 12 to 14; with 58 and 68 members respectively, leaderboard coverage remains almost unchanged at 20.7% and 20.6%.

DIE’s lower point total and S105’s approximately unchanged total persist at all tested thresholds of 500, 1,000, 5,000 and 10,000 points. These are trends in system-recorded points. They do not establish less effort or a smaller strategic contribution.

## Data sources

Ranks 1–720 were recorded from 79 ranking images. The continuation from rank 721 was deliberately not photographed; its scores are at most 250 points, below the analysis threshold. The 65 captured entries below 1,000 points remain in the source data but are excluded from metrics and the displayed comparison.

25 alliance screenshots from 18 October cover 20 distinct S105 overviews. 19 can be identified unambiguously; a notification obscures the alliance title in one. That overview is excluded from membership ratios. Names and special characters were additionally transcribed directly from the images; some similar-looking characters remain flagged.

The ranking screenshots are from 17 October, while alliance overviews are from the following morning. Membership counts are snapshots, not a fixed player cohort. The historical view for 10 October remains unfiltered; only the weekly comparison was recalculated using the same threshold in both weeks.
