The rankings contain more players from S105; S97 has more system-recorded points. This describes the captured point totals, not the strategic contribution to the conquest.

## What these data can and cannot tell us

This analysis shows only the event points recorded by the game system at the time of each screenshot. It cannot reliably establish the actual strategic contribution of individual players, alliances or servers to Capital Conquest.

Fighting before the conquest may prevent opponents from taking up their positions and make a substantial contribution without earning corresponding event points. Conversely, attacks after the actual conquest phase, for example on bases engaged in gathering, may earn high event points without contributing to the conquest objective. The rankings do not show whether or to what extent either occurred.

High point totals therefore do not establish high strategic value; low or missing points do not establish a lack of effort. Leaderboard coverage describes only the players recorded statistically. It does not assess actual participation, support, commitment or performance.

## Limits of interpretation

These data cannot establish battle success, territorial control, the importance of individual actions, playing time, losses or support outside the scoring system. Recommendations on positioning, roles or commitment require additional observations and battle reports.

## Data sources

The complete photographed rankings contain 563 players and 466,531,316 points across 60 screenshots, plus 20 S105 alliance overviews. The screenshots were taken around 16:49 on 10 October 2026; this does not establish a simultaneous final battle result.

The direct server comparison covers 560 players from S105 and S97. Three additional entries from S108, S110 and S112, totalling 29,028 points, are excluded from it. Membership figures are snapshots; averages and medians are rounded to whole points.

This historical view includes all recorded entries without a minimum-points filter. A weekly comparison must apply the same minimum to both weeks. Such a filter defines a statistical selection, not verified participation.
