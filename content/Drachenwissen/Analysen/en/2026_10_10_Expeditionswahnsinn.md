# Capital Conquest analysis for 10 October 2026

**S105 has more players in the rankings, while S97 earns substantially more points. Participation is an important area for our alliance DIE to improve: our 15 recorded participants achieve a higher points return than the S105 average.**

## Comparing S105 and S97

| Metric | S105 | S97 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Participants in the rankings | 318 | 242 |
| Share of both servers' participants | 56.8% | 43.2% |
| Total points | 184,974,397 | 281,527,891 |
| Share of both servers' points | 39.7% | 60.3% |
| Average per participant | 581,680 | 1,163,338 |
| Median per participant | 296,032 | 1,192,870 |

S105 has 76 more participants than S97. Nevertheless, S97 leads by around 96.6 million points. On average, S97 earns almost twice as many points per recorded participant.

The median reveals the difference across the field. It is the middle value in a server's rankings. On S97, it is around 1.19 million points, roughly four times the S105 median. The lead therefore does not rely solely on a few top contributions: S97's middle-ranked participant also earns considerably more points.

The rankings alone cannot tell us whether player strength, time spent, assigned roles or other factors caused this difference. In the next analysis, it would be useful to compare the points data with our practical experience during the event.

## Which alliances contribute the points

On S105, IGGG leads with 44.05 million points, followed by GGR with 40.55 million and BTs with 22.50 million. Together, they earn **57.9% of all S105 points**. TuT follows with 21.72 million and Lrs with 13.96 million points.

On S97, lga leads with 58.00 million, KKL with 48.00 million and KVIZ with 44.08 million points. Their combined server share is 53.3%. Behind them, CrZ contributes another 43.42 million points. S97 therefore has four alliances above 40 million points, compared with two on S105.

Our planning should therefore consider both the number of participants and their share of the alliance. Membership counts are unavailable for S97, so participation rates cannot be compared between the servers.

## Our alliance DIE

We earn **9,564,831 points**, placing sixth among S105 alliances by total points. Our contribution represents 5.2% of our server's points.

15 of 58 members appear in the rankings. At 25.9%, our participation is seventh among the 20 alliances with known membership counts. The remaining 43 members have no ranking entry in this snapshot; this says nothing about their availability or their roles during the event.

Our participants average 637,655 points, around 9.6% above the S105 average. The median is 762,697 points. Our middle-ranked participant therefore also earns substantially more points than S105's middle-ranked participant.

Markus78 makes the largest individual contribution with 1,569,148 points, followed by Anthlan with 1,442,449. The five highest-scoring players earn 58.6% of our alliance points, and the top ten earn 93.7%. Several key players contribute to our result, but it is clearly concentrated among those ten.

## Areas to work on for the next event

A useful first goal for DIE would be to involve more members with a measurable contribution. **With membership unchanged at 58, 20 participants would mean a participation rate of 34.5%.** That would be five additional participants and roughly the current BTs rate. It does not imply any particular additional score.

Before the next event, we should clarify who can participate, which roles suit their capabilities and what support they need. Afterwards, we can check whether wider participation also improves total points and the median. Merely appearing in the rankings and making a larger points contribution should be considered separately.

For the server comparison, the central question is how more of our already recorded participants can achieve higher contributions. IGGG and TuT's experience with broad participation, and the high-scoring alliances' experience during their deployments, could be useful.

## Data basis

The complete rankings comprise 563 players and 466,531,316 points across 60 screenshots, plus 20 alliance overviews from S105. The screenshots were taken on 10 October 2026 at around 16:49 and describe the points at that moment, rather than the outcome of the battle.

The direct comparison includes only the 560 players from S105 and S97. Three further entries from S108, S110 and S112, totalling 29,028 points, are excluded from that comparison. Participation here means players with a ranking entry divided by the alliance's membership in the snapshot. Averages and medians are rounded to whole points.
