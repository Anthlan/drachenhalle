# Observations on Capital Conquest, 24 October 2026

At a minimum of 1,000 system points, the first snapshot contains 652 players and 676,404,845 points; the later snapshot contains 672 uniquely recorded players and 717,124,843 points. Capture times and gaps must be considered.

## Two snapshots

Immediately after conquest: S105 has 242 players and 343,652,937 points; S109 has 410 players and 332,751,908 points. After the gathering phase: S105 has 248 players and 365,680,674 points; S109 has 423 players and 351,376,169 points. One S112 player with 68,000 points is listed separately.

## Comparable phase data

650 uniquely matched players reach at least 1,000 points in both snapshots. Their combined points rise from 673,728,420 to 708,597,909, an increase of 34,869,489. One duplicate was removed; two earlier players could not be uniquely matched later. Comparing overall totals therefore mixes point changes with differences in coverage.

## Context compared with the previous week

On 17 October, S105 recorded 182,614,549 points at the same threshold; immediately after conquest on 24 October, 343,652,937. The recorded total is higher. Different capture times and the opponent changing from S107 to S109 prevent conclusions about better performance or a greater contribution to conquest.

## Our alliance DIE

16 players reach at least 1,000 points in both snapshots. Their total rises from 13,477,421 to 13,934,211, an increase of 456,790. Against the later membership of 70, this gives a leaderboard coverage of 22.9%. This ratio does not assess actual participation.

## Data sources

These data show system points only. Fighting before conquest may significantly disrupt enemy deployment without earning points here. Attacking gathering bases after conquest may produce many points without contributing to the conquest itself. The records establish neither individual effort nor the battle outcome.
