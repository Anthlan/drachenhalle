# Zombie Siege: defend the waves together

During Zombie Siege, several waves attack our bases one after another. Unlike a normal attack, an active shield does not help here. What matters is setting a defence, having enough hospital capacity and coordinating with the alliance in advance.

The announcement explains the process so both active participants and members who want to sit out this time know what to do.

![Zombie Siege – process and participation](../../../../Drachenwissen/Eventankuendigungen/ZombieBelagerung_Ablauf_Teilnahme.png)

## How participation works

If you want to participate, set your defence before the start. Each wave you survive improves your individual performance and your contribution to the alliance result. If your defence is breached, the siege ends for you.

Losses during the event go to the hospital; soldiers do not die. Even so, check that you have enough hospital capacity before starting.

## Sitting out or receiving support

If you do not want to participate, leave your defence empty. Your event then ends after the first wave, with no further preparation required.

If you need support defending, ask before the start if possible. This gives the alliance time to coordinate help. Anthlan and the R4 officers are your first points of contact.

> **Before sending:** `[UHRZEIT]` must be replaced with the local time announced for this event. The existing illustration shows the version for **21:00**.
