# Alliance Ambush: prepare well and limit losses

Alliance Ambush depends on as many members as possible being online together and deploying their troops carefully. This announcement therefore reminds everyone about the start time and about managing hospital capacity, losses and healing thoughtfully.

For the detailed event strategy, including personal rally quests and the shared x10 goal, see the [Alliance Ambush tip](../../Tipps/en/Tipp_07_AllianzHinterhalt.md). This announcement is deliberately shorter and intended for use immediately before the event.

## Before the start

- Check the announced time and be online a few minutes early.
- Keep the troops and marches you intend to use available.
- Make as much room as possible in your hospital.
- Follow the event coordinators' current guidance on difficulty and team strength.

## During the ambush

Careful deployment is better than continuing to fight blindly. Check your hospital and losses regularly. If the selected level is being completed safely, adjusted formations can reduce unnecessary losses. If total damage is barely sufficient, completing the event successfully takes priority.

While online during the event, heal troops in small batches. This lets you use alliance help more frequently and return soldiers to action faster.

> **Before sending:** `[UHRZEIT]` must be replaced with the local time announced for this event.
