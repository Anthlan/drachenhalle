# Alliance Duel: use supplies at the right time

Before our duel day, we remind ourselves of the daily objective, a matching Arms Race phase and enough free radar capacity. We only add specific times after checking the current game displays.

Our collection themes: Radar Exploration on Monday, Base Construction on Tuesday, Technology Research on Wednesday, Hero Training on Thursday and full Military Preparation on Friday. Enemy Attack follows on Saturday. Radar completions are confirmed to count on Monday, Wednesday and Friday.

## Copy template: prepare on Tuesday for Wednesday

```text
DlE • Tomorrow is Technology Research! Keep research data, research speedups and fighter component chests ready. Radar quests count tomorrow too: keep valid quests, but make enough room before the next refill. Combine research with a matching Arms Race phase where possible; check the task list and deadline.
```

## Copy template: prepare on Thursday for Friday

```text
DlE • Tomorrow is Military Preparation! Plan soldier training and training speedups; construction, research and radar also count. Keep valid radar quests and make room before refills. Use the matching Arms Race window for each action and coordinate extra effort towards our daily victory.
```

Read more: [Duel, Arms Race and radar](/drachenhalle/en/docs/tipp-14-allianz-wettkampf-punkte-planen/). Calculate: [Duel planner](/drachenhalle/en/tools/wettkampfplaner/).

## Copy template: before the duel day

```text
DlE • Alliance Duel: plan your daily target and keep matching supplies ready. Before spending them, check whether the same action also counts in Arms Race. Keep radar quests only if they remain valid until the scoring day; make enough room before each refill. Check values and time windows in the game. Find help in the Dragon Hall duel planner.
```

## Copy template: before the radar refill

```text
DlE • Saving radar: check your current quests, storage limit and next refill amount. Free only the necessary space and keep the rest for the matching duel day, provided the quests remain valid until then. Check whether completing or claiming a quest frees storage and awards points.
```

All templates are individual chat messages of fewer than 1,000 characters. They deliberately contain no unverified server time.
