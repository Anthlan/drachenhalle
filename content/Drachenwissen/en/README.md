# Dragon Knowledge

On the website, **Dragon Knowledge** brings together tips, strategies and everyday gameplay information in three main collections:

- [Tips](../Tipps/en/README.md) – our central gameplay knowledge collection; use the **Type** and **Topic** filters
- [Events](../Events/en/README.md) – limited-time event guides with short versions, detailed strategies and budget recommendations
- [Event announcements](../Eventankuendigungen/en/README.md) – reusable notices for upcoming alliance events with copy templates

Tutorials and alliance rules and organisation are separate main areas. Their source files are therefore under [`Tutorials`](../../Tutorials/en/README.md) and [`Allianz`](../../Allianz/en/README.md).

The source folders `Anleitungen`, `Strategien` and `Analysen` remain for existing filing references and possible future use, but do not yet appear as separate website sections. Guide, Strategy and Optimisation are instead maintained as type tags directly on tip files.

Store new public gameplay content as numbered tip files under [`Tipps`](../Tipps/en/README.md). The website automatically reads their content and metadata during the build.
