# 💡 Tip #9 – Relocate for free through Raid

## Key points

- Open **Alliance Competition → Raid → Go** to travel to the opposing server for free.
- When traveling, you can freely choose your destination – similar to an Advanced Relocator.
- The outward trip starts a **5-minute cooldown**. However, this does not block the return trip.
- Using **“Return”** immediately takes you back to your own server for free.
- On returning, you can also choose a new position for your base.
- **Strategically important:** The relocator also works while troops are outside your base. No prior recall is needed.
- This lets you reposition your base without an Advanced Relocator while Raid is available.

> **Remember:** Jump to the opponent for free, then immediately select a new home position through “Return” – even with troops outside your base.

![Tip 9 – Relocate for free through Raid](../../../../media/knowledge/Tipps/Tipp_09_GratisUmziehenPerUeberfall.png)

## What is this tip for?

Raid is normally a cross-server combat mechanic. However, its outward and return trips can also serve as a free relocator: travel to the opposing server, then use “Return” to immediately choose a new position on your home server. The cooldown started by the outward trip runs independently of this return option.

Unlike many normal teleport situations, you do not need to wait until all troops are back in your base. Free relocation through Raid can also start while troops are outside. This keeps the method tactically useful even when your units are currently marching.

It is particularly useful when you want to:

- move closer to your alliance,
- leave an unfavorable or dangerous position,
- organize your formation for an upcoming event,
- occupy a spot in an alliance area,
- or save Advanced Relocators for situations when Raid is unavailable.

The crucial difference from a normal server transfer: you do not permanently move to another server. The trip happens within the time-limited Raid and ends by returning to your home server.

## How free relocation works

The method consists of two free position selections: an outward trip to the opposing server and a subsequent return trip to your own server.

```text
Alliance Competition → Raid → Go
            ↓
Choose a position on the opposing server
            ↓
Select “Return” – immediately available
            ↓
choose a new position on your own server

Separate note: The outward trip starts a cooldown
of 5 minutes. Returning is unaffected.
```

### 1. Travel to the opposing server

1. Open **Alliance Competition**.
2. Switch to **Raid**.
3. Tap **“Go”**.
4. Choose an available destination on the opposing server.
5. Before confirming, check that no relocator or other currency is required.
6. Confirm the jump.

According to the available player experience, you can freely choose the position on the opposing server. The outward trip therefore works like an Advanced Relocator – but only within the Raid event.

### 2. Return immediately for free

1. After the outward trip, open Raid again.
2. Select **“Return”** – you do not need to wait five minutes.
3. Find the desired new position on your home server.
4. Check the surroundings before confirming the return.

According to the documented player experience, the return trip is also free. Crucially, do not simply confirm any empty spot: the coordinates selected when returning become your base’s new position.

### Understand the cooldown correctly

Jumping to the opponent starts a **5-minute cooldown**, but it is **not a waiting period before returning**. “Return” remains immediately available.

The timer is therefore not shown as a separate step between the outward and return trips. Check the in-game display to determine which other Raid action it restricts in the current version.

> **Important:** Outward trip → immediate return is possible. The displayed cooldown runs separately and does not block “Return”.

## Strategically important: Troops may be outside your base

Relocation through Raid works even while troops are outside your base. You **do not need to recall them** before the outward trip or wait for them to return.

This is strategically valuable:

- Distant troops do not delay repositioning.
- You can immediately take a favorable opportunity to relocate for free.
- A planned position change need not be postponed solely because troops are deployed elsewhere.
- Advanced Relocators can still be saved in this situation.

This statement concerns the requirement for using the relocator: troops outside your base do not prevent its use. It does not automatically establish where those troops march after relocation or how an active march is handled. After repositioning, check the affected troops’ displays and return routes.

> **Practical rule:** Troops outside? No problem for this relocator – prior recall is unnecessary.

## Why this saves an Advanced Relocator

An Advanced Relocator allows you to freely choose a destination. Raid achieves the same effect relevant to this tip through the return mechanic: you can place your base at a new available position on your home server without using the item.

| Method | Position freely selectable | Cost | Availability |
| --- | :---: | --- | --- |
| Advanced Relocator | Yes | 1 relocator | when the item is available and teleporting is permitted |
| Raid outward trip | Yes | free according to player observations | only while Raid is available |
| Return from Raid | Yes | free according to player observations | immediately after the outward trip; independent of the cooldown |

Raid therefore does not replace the Advanced Relocator entirely. It is an event-specific saving opportunity. A normal relocator remains important for spontaneous moves outside the event.

For more on regular relocators, see **Tip #4 – Teleport correctly**.

## Choose a good new position

The free return is especially valuable when you plan the destination beforehand. Consider:

- **Proximity to the alliance:** Short routes make help, reinforcements and joint actions easier.
- **Available space:** Check that neighboring members will also have enough room.
- **Distance from opponents:** A visually appealing position may be strategically poor if strong opponents are directly beside it.
- **Proximity to targets:** During upcoming events, short marches may matter more than a perfect formation.
- **Resource tiles and routes:** Do not block important routes, and review the area around the planned alliance formation.
- **Coordination:** Ask R4/R5 about planned rows, sectors or gathering points before placing your base.

If several members use the method simultaneously, the alliance should mark desired positions beforehand or coordinate them in chat. Otherwise, gaps or competing claims to preferred positions can easily arise.

## Useful situations

### Move back to the alliance

If your base is far outside the alliance area, use the return to rejoin the shared formation. This saves an Advanced Relocator and subsequently shortens many marches.

### Correct a poor position

Even minor misplacements can be disruptive – for example, a base blocking a row or standing too far from the center. The free return lets you correct this without spending a valuable item to move a few tiles.

### Regroup before an event

If the alliance changes its formation before a combat or gathering event, Raid can provide a good opportunity to reorganize. However, the destination should already be decided before jumping.

### Keep relocators as an emergency reserve

Advanced Relocators are particularly valuable when rapid relocation is needed outside Raid. Using free returns for planned moves preserves more items for real emergencies.

## Checklist before jumping

- Is **Raid** currently open and available to you?
- Does the confirmation actually show a free transfer?
- Do you know the desired destination area on your home server?
- Is sufficient free space available there?
- Has the new position been coordinated with the alliance?
- Are troops outside the base? This does not block the relocator; recall is unnecessary.
- Have you accounted for the **5-minute cooldown** on other Raid actions without confusing it with a return restriction?
- Have ongoing marches, battles or other restrictions in the current menu been considered?
- Have you checked the current shield and buff notices?

The last point is deliberately phrased as a check: available public sources do not sufficiently document whether or how peace shields, buffs, marches or ongoing battles are handled during Raid. Do not rely on additional claims; check the current in-game display before confirming.

## Common mistakes

- **Confusing Raid with permanent server transfer:** This is a time-limited journey to the opponent and back.
- **Treating the cooldown as a return restriction:** This is wrong – “Return” can be selected immediately.
- **Recalling every troop as a precaution:** This is unnecessary for this relocator; troops outside your base do not block it.
- **Jumping without a return position:** Decide beforehand where your base should stand afterward.
- **Confirming too quickly:** Check surroundings, alliance formation and coordinates before completing the return.
- **Planning to use the method at any time:** It works only when the relevant Raid is available.
- **Using a relocator without checking:** For this method, go through Alliance Competition and Raid, rather than the normal relocator menu.
- **Assuming additional effects:** Claims about shields, buffs or active marches must be checked in the current game version.
- **Treating the timer as permanent:** Updates may change the cooldown; the in-game display takes priority.

## Limitations and open questions

Publicly available Raid information is limited. An external community source confirms that players can teleport to the opposing server during Alliance Competition and fight there. However, the free choice of position on both trips and the specific 5-minute cooldown described here come from supplied current player experience and alliance documentation.

Before use, check:

- whether both trips are still displayed as free,
- whether positions are still freely selectable on both servers,
- whether the timer still lasts 5 minutes,
- which other Raid action the timer currently restricts – according to available player experience, returning is not one of them,
- how troops outside your base appear after repositioning and which return routes the game shows,
- what restrictions apply to ongoing marches or battles,
- and how active shields or buffs are handled in the current version.

## Sources and reliability

- **Primary basis:** supplied current player description and existing alliance illustration of Raid.
- **Public confirmation of cross-server travel:** [Interserver War – IMPh community overview](https://imph.info/interserver-war) describes Alliance Competition and teleporting to the opposing server.
- **Not fully verified publicly:** free outward and return trips, free position selection when returning and the specific 5-minute cooldown. For these details, the current in-game display takes priority.
