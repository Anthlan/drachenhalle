# 💡 Tip #5 – Freight Train

## Key points

- **Train conductor:** R4/R5 appoint a member to prepare the Freight Train. The role is not limited to a particular rank.
- **Plan a gold train:** Refreshing cargo costs **5 Convoy Contracts**. After **5 refreshes**, or **25 contracts**, a gold train with four UR carriages is guaranteed.
- **Entire cargo changes:** A refresh rerolls all the cargo, rather than individual carriages. A carriage that already has UR quality can become worse again before the guarantee.
- **Remember defense:** Before departure, the conductor sets up **3 defending teams**.
- **Passengers cannot be chosen:** Each carriage carries at most **5 members**. If too many apply, the game selects randomly – neither the conductor nor R4/R5 can intervene.
- **Collect contracts:** Convoy Contracts come from events, tasks and activities; they are not available in the alliance shop.

> **Remember:** Secure 25 contracts first, refresh five times, and set up three strong defending teams before departure.

## What is this tip for?

This tip explains the entire Freight Train process: from appointing the conductor through applications and voluntary contract donations to refreshing cargo and setting up defense. The aim is to use scarce Convoy Contracts predictably, avoid misunderstandings about passenger selection, and send the train on its journey with valuable cargo and good protection.

The article is particularly useful for:

- **R4/R5**, who appoint a conductor and coordinate departure,
- the **conductor**, who prepares cargo and defense,
- **passengers**, who can apply and contribute Convoy Contracts,
- and all members who want to understand why a guaranteed gold train requires 25 contracts.

## Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Task | What can this role decide? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **R4/R5** | Appoint the conductor and coordinate the process within the alliance | who becomes conductor |
| **Conductor** | Refresh cargo and set up three defending teams before departure | how often to refresh and which teams defend |
| **Passengers** | Apply for a carriage and voluntarily donate contracts when boarding | whether to contribute contracts |
| **Game system** | Select applicants when oversubscribed | who actually boards if too many members apply |

The crucial distinction: the conductor manages the train but **cannot choose preferred passengers**. According to the available in-game documentation, R4/R5 also have no influence on random selection.

## 1. Appoint the conductor

R4 or R5 appoint the conductor. Any alliance member can generally take on this task; their rank is not decisive.

Since the conductor handles the crucial preparations, the alliance should choose using practical criteria:

- Will the member reliably be online before departure?
- Do they understand the alliance’s goal – for example, a gold train or economical contract use?
- Can they field three suitable defending teams?
- Has the number of Convoy Contracts available for the train, including passenger donations, been agreed?

> **Practical recommendation:** Do more than name someone: agree in advance whether refreshing all the way to a gold train is the firm goal. This prevents stopping after several refreshes because the last contracts are missing.

## 2. Passengers apply

Each of the four carriages holds a maximum of **5 passengers**. If all four are open to passengers, that gives up to **20 seats** in total.

If a carriage receives more than five applications, the game selects randomly. Two important rules follow:

- Applying does not guarantee a seat when oversubscribed.
- The conductor and R4/R5 cannot favor or exclude individual members.

Selection should therefore not be seen as a personal decision by alliance leadership. Anyone not selected for an overbooked carriage has been excluded by the game mechanic.

It is not documented whether application timing, VIP status or other characteristics affect the current selection probability. The supplied in-game illustration describes selection only as random.

## 3. Donate Convoy Contracts when boarding

The supplied in-game illustration shows that selected passengers can donate Convoy Contracts when boarding. These donations help the conductor refresh cargo more often.

Donating is **voluntary**:

- Passengers donating Convoy Contracts board and increase the available stock.
- According to the in-game illustration, those who only respond or close the dialog without donating also board.
- A donation is therefore not a boarding requirement and should not be confused with random applicant selection.

Advance coordination still makes sense for the alliance. If several selected passengers contribute small amounts, the conductor does not need to supply all 25 contracts for five refreshes alone.

## 4. Refresh cargo

One refresh costs **5 Convoy Contracts**. After five refreshes, a gold train is guaranteed; all four carriages then receive UR status.

| Number of refreshes | Total contracts required | Guaranteed result |
| ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 5 | Cargo is completely rerolled |
| 2 | 10 | no gold-train guarantee yet |
| 3 | 15 | no gold-train guarantee yet |
| 4 | 20 | no gold-train guarantee yet |
| 5 | 25 | **Gold train guaranteed: all 4 carriages UR** |

### Why individual good carriages are not secured progress

Every refresh **rerolls all carriages**. Existing qualities are not individually locked. A UR carriage from an earlier result can therefore receive a lower quality on the next refresh.

This has two consequences:

1. Until the guaranteed fifth round, a refresh is not a pure improvement but a new roll of the entire cargo.
2. Anyone specifically aiming for a gold train should budget the full **25 contracts before starting**. Otherwise, the alliance may end up with worse intermediate cargo and insufficient contracts to reach the guarantee.

Securing 25 contracts beforehand is a strategic recommendation derived from the documented costs and complete reroll. It is not an additional game rule.

## 5. Set up three defending teams

Before departure, the conductor sets up **3 defending teams**. The Freight Train can be attacked or plundered during the journey, so weak or incomplete teams put the contracts already invested at unnecessary risk.

Before starting, the conductor should check all three teams:

- Are the strongest reasonably available heroes assigned?
- Are sufficient soldiers allocated?
- Do the heroes, troop types and available formation bonuses fit together?
- Has a weak everyday or gathering team been selected accidentally?
- Are all three defense slots actually occupied?

Publicly available update notes show that the Freight Train recently gained **squad-bonus selection during plundering attacks**, and its interface and rules have been revised several times. Check directly in the game which bonuses are currently available and exactly how they work.

## 6. Where do Convoy Contracts come from?

The supplied in-game documentation lists three sources:

- **Events**
- **Tasks**
- **Activities**

Convoy Contracts are not available in the **alliance shop**. The alliance therefore cannot simply cover a missing balance with alliance coins immediately before departure.

A specific, permanently valid list of all events and tasks with their quantities is not reliably documented publicly. Rewards and event periods can change, so the current in-game reward preview is the authoritative source.

### Sensible stock planning

- Do not spend contracts without coordination when the alliance plans a gold train.
- Check before departure how many contracts are actually available for the train.
- Tell passengers early that they can voluntarily donate contracts when boarding.
- Plan with **25 contracts actually available**, not merely promised donations.

## The complete process

1. **R4/R5 appoint the conductor.**
2. **Members apply** for carriages; each carriage has at most five seats.
3. **The game selects randomly when oversubscribed.** The conductor and R4/R5 cannot control selection.
4. **Selected passengers board** and can voluntarily donate Convoy Contracts.
5. **The conductor refreshes the cargo.** Each refresh costs five contracts and rerolls all carriages.
6. **After five refreshes**, or 25 contracts, a gold train with four UR carriages is guaranteed.
7. **The conductor sets up three defending teams.**
8. **The Freight Train departs.** Attacks or plundering attempts may occur during the journey.
9. **Rewards are distributed after completion.** Their quality depends on the prepared cargo and current game rules.

## Recommended alliance process for a gold train

### Before applications open

- Choose the conductor and planned departure time.
- Announce alliance-wide that 25 Convoy Contracts are the shared target.
- Clarify who can contribute contracts.

### Before refreshing cargo

- Count the contracts actually available.
- Count only donations that are reliably available.
- Decide whether the alliance can complete all five refreshes.

### Before departure

- Check that all four carriages show UR status after the fifth refresh.
- Thoroughly check all three defending teams.
- Review current squad bonuses or plundering rules in the game.
- Only then start.

## Common mistakes

- **Starting with too few contracts:** After several rerolls, the means to reach the guaranteed fifth refresh are missing.
- **Assuming UR carriages are locked:** The next refresh rerolls all cargo; individual UR carriages are not automatically retained.
- **Trying to assign passengers manually:** With more than five applicants per carriage, the game decides randomly.
- **Confusing donations with the right to board:** According to the in-game illustration, selected passengers can board without donating.
- **Optimizing only cargo:** A valuable train still needs three useful defending teams.
- **Looking for contracts in the alliance shop:** They are not available there according to the documented state.
- **Counting promised rather than available donations:** If a contribution fails to arrive, the gold-train guarantee may be missed.
