# 💡 Tip #14 – Plan our Alliance Competition week

## Key points

- **Monday:** Radar Exploration – radar, stamina, fighter materials and gathering.
- **Tuesday:** Base Construction – construction, survivor recruitment, UR missions and UR trucks.
- **Wednesday:** Technology Research – research, research data, component chests and radar.
- **Thursday:** Hero Training – shards, recruitment, skill books and hero EXP.
- **Friday:** Full Military Preparation – training, construction, research and radar.
- **Saturday:** Enemy Attack – our combat day after the five gathering phases.
- **Radar counts on Monday, Wednesday and Friday.** Prepare stock on Tuesday and Thursday; check space and quest expiry before every refill.

In the [Competition Planner](/drachenhalle/en/tools/wettkampfplaner/), choose a day and enter your planned quantities. Point values from our game screenshots are already provided and can be adjusted.

## What is this tip for?

We assign our stock to the five fixed collection themes and decide when to use it. Then we check the corresponding Arms Race phase so the same action counts in both events where possible. Saturday is treated as an additional combat day.

## Our week: What should we save for each day?

| Day | Theme | Prepare these | Check Arms Race |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Radar Exploration | radar quests, stamina, fighter parts, combat chips, Wingman chests; gathering teams | Stamina/Radar for stamina use; Hero Development for hero EXP |
| Tuesday | Base Construction | construction completions, construction speedups, survivor recruitment; UR missions and UR trucks | Base Development for construction actions |
| Wednesday | Technology Research | research data, research completions, research speedups, component chests, radar quests | Technology Research for explicitly listed research actions |
| Thursday | Hero Training | UR/SSR/SR shards, hero recruitment, skill EXP books, hero EXP | Hero Development for the specifically listed action |
| Friday | Full Military Preparation | soldier training, training speedups, construction/research and corresponding speedups, radar quests | Training, construction or research depending on the action |
| Saturday | Enemy Attack | coordinated combat, shields, healing, remaining speedups; UR missions and UR trucks | Training, construction or research depending on the speedup |

Days follow our confirmed Monday-to-Friday rhythm; phase names and scoring are evidenced in the supplied game screenshots. The Arms Race column is a **checking aid**, not a verified task or time list. Similar headings are insufficient: check whether starting, completing, consuming an item or collecting a reward counts.

## How to use the planner

1. Choose the day you want to prepare stock for.
2. Enter your current score. Choose the next milestone, one of the nine chest thresholds or your own daily target.
3. For each action, enter only the quantity you intend to use. Enter speedups separately as days (d), hours (h) and minutes (m); the planner automatically combines them. Fighter component chests are compactly grouped by level, with a separate quantity for each level.
4. Check the point gap. Add another suitable action if needed or reduce use when the target is already covered.
5. Before actual use, check the matching Arms Race window and remaining competition time.

The planner adds several actions. It does not automatically rank your stock by value or recommend consuming everything. Plans for different days are independent: do not enter the same available stock several times if it is already reserved for another day.

Preset numbers are **displayed account values**, not confirmed base values for every member. Green numbers may include personal bonuses. If your game display differs, adjust the relevant score in the tool; no additional bonus is automatically added.

## Example: Prepare Wednesday

Using the screenshot values, 20 radar quests give **483,000 points**, 1,000 research data give **640,000 points**, and 60 minutes of research speedups give **7,560 points**. Together that is **1,130,560 points**. Points from a research completion are not included; add the expected power increase if the game display scores both actions separately.

For a 1,200,000-point target, this example is still 69,440 points short. Three further radar quests at 24,150 points each would close the gap. Before use, check your actual scoring and whether completing quests is necessary to create space for new ones.

## Save radar: Which days matter?

The screenshots award **24,150 points per radar quest completed on Monday, Wednesday and Friday**. No radar-completion score is listed for Tuesday, Thursday or Saturday.

| Current day | Next radar day | Our approach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Wednesday | Meet today’s target first, then keep remaining valid quests |
| Tuesday | Wednesday | Prepare stock and check space before refills |
| Wednesday | Friday | Meet today’s target first, then keep remaining valid quests |
| Thursday | Friday | Prepare stock and check space before refills |
| Friday | Monday | Meet today’s target first; keep only quests with sufficient remaining validity |
| Saturday / Sunday | Monday | Prepare for Monday without blocking new quests or allowing old ones to expire |

**When should you stop?** Once today’s target and the necessary free space for the next refill are covered, you can save the rest for the next radar day. With several refills, recheck before each one. A specific saving time still requires confirmed refill times, quest validity and storage rules.

Example using hypothetical storage values: with 28 saved quests, a limit of 35 and ten new quests, you must free three spaces. You can keep 25 quests; after refilling, there would be 35. The supplied material does not document whether completion or reward collection frees space and triggers scoring.

According to Monday’s screenshot, radar gathering quests **do not count** toward the additional food, metal or oil gathering score. Do not count their resources twice. Separate radar-completion points must be considered independently.

## Distinguish daily points from victory points

The phase overview shows **1 victory point on Monday**, **2 each from Tuesday through Friday**, and **4 on Saturday**. These are weekly-scoring weights, not points for individual actions. Saturday therefore has significant weight; we jointly decide which stock or deployments it requires.

The three added reward screenshots now show all **nine milestones**. The planner offers them as selectable targets and distinguishes thresholds already reached from those covered only by your planned use.

| Milestone | Required action points |
| --- | ---: |
| 1 | 38,000 |
| 2 | 145,000 |
| 3 | 550,000 |
| 4 | 650,000 |
| 5 | 1,020,000 |
| 6 | 2,280,000 |

Thresholds are cumulative: milestone 3 requires 550,000 total points, not the sum of the first three thresholds. If you already have 145,000 points, another 405,000 are needed. “Next milestone” follows the current score; planned use does not automatically move the selected target.

Sources: `20261006_202428000_iOS.jpg`, `20261006_202445000_iOS.jpg` and `20261006_202454000_iOS.jpg`. Diamond symbols above the chests are not interpreted here as guaranteed diamond payouts; actual chest contents are not expanded. Check availability, unlocks and differing account thresholds in-game. Reaching a point threshold does not automatically mean the reward has been collected.

Saturday lists general eliminations, eliminations in “specific combat” and losses separately. It is not established whether one elimination triggers both categories simultaneously. Do not automatically count it twice. Losses are a cost; we do not deliberately create them for points.
