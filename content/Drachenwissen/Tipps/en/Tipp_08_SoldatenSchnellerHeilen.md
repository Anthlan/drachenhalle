# 💡 Tip #8 – Heal soldiers faster

## Key points

- **Heal in small batches while online:** Do not automatically start healing the entire hospital.
- **Request alliance help immediately:** Every timely help reduces the remaining healing time.
- **Start with 20–40 soldiers:** This range often suits an initial test; the healing time actually displayed matters more afterward.
- **Calculate instant-heal time:** Multiply your personal **Help Time** by the number of members likely to help immediately. The healing batch should take no longer than this.
- **Repeat immediately:** As soon as one batch finishes, start the next and request help again.
- **Plan differently offline:** Before a longer absence, set a large batch whose healing time roughly matches your offline period.

> **Remember:** Help Time × immediate helpers = maximum healing time for an instant-heal batch.

![Tip 8 – Heal soldiers faster](../../../../media/knowledge/Tipps/Tipp_08_SoldatenSchnellerHeilen.png)

## What is this tip for?

This tip shows how small healing batches and repeated alliance help can often make large numbers of wounded soldiers combat-ready much faster. Instead of setting one healing task lasting several hours, divide the stock into short sections that alliance help can substantially reduce.

The method is particularly useful:

- after major PvP battles,
- when the hospital is heavily occupied,
- while further battles are still taking place,
- when several alliance members are online simultaneously,
- or when you want to save diamonds and healing speedups.

However, it requires your active involvement. If you are about to go offline, a long healing task matching your absence works better.

## Why small healing batches work

After starting healing, you can request alliance help. Active members then press “Help”, reducing the remaining time.

How much time each help subtracts is shown in your personal buff values under **Help Time**. This value consists partly of building bonuses, alliance technology and survivors. The **Help Limit** also caps how many helps a single action can receive.

However, planning should not automatically use the entire Help Limit. What matters is how many members are currently online and likely to respond **immediately** to your request.

The principle is:

```text
small healing batch → request help → helpers reduce the timer →
batch ends → start next healing batch
```

The increased speed does not come from one stronger healing bonus, but from using the help process again for every new batch.

## Online healing step by step

1. Open the hospital and check wounded numbers and tiers.
2. Open your buff overview and read **Help Time** and **Help Limit**.
3. Realistically estimate how many members will help immediately – not merely how many appear online.
4. Calculate your instant-heal time from this.
5. Select enough soldiers for the displayed healing time to fall just below that result.
6. Start healing.
7. Request **alliance help immediately**.
8. As soon as the batch finishes, immediately start the next and request help again.
9. Repeat until all desired soldiers are healed or you want to go offline.

> **Important:** Press the alliance-help button again after every newly started healing batch. A previous help request does not automatically apply to subsequent batches.

## Calculate instant-heal time

The formula is:

```text
instant-heal time = Help Time × expected immediate helpers
```

The number of helpers used must not exceed your personal Help Limit:

```text
helper count used = the smaller of
“expected immediate helpers” and “Help Limit”
```

Optimal healing time is therefore **not a universal fixed value**. It changes when your Help Time rises or more or fewer members can respond immediately.

Use the top total value in each case for the calculation – not just one of the individual bonuses listed below it.

- **Help Time:** 742 seconds per help

The Help Time components total 742 seconds. This equals **12 minutes and 22 seconds per help**.

If you expect **5 immediate helps**:

```text
742 seconds × 5 helpers = 3,710 seconds
3,710 seconds = 1 hour, 1 minute and 50 seconds
```

For this profile, five helps can immediately complete a healing batch lasting at most **1:01:50**. In practice, allow a small safety margin – for example, a displayed healing time of approximately **60 minutes**.

> **Example decision:** If five reliable helpers are online, select enough soldiers for the timer to show just under an hour. Then start healing, request help, and immediately set the next batch after the five helps.

## Soldier count or calculated healing time – which guideline is better?

Soldier count is easy to remember, but healing time is more precise.

| Guideline | Advantage | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **20–40 soldiers** | quick to set and a good first test | different soldier tiers and bonuses produce different times |
| **30–50 minutes of healing** | useful general community starting point | does not yet account precisely for personal Help Time and actual helper count |
| **Help Time × expected helpers** | gives your personal instant-heal timer | works only if the members counted actually help immediately |

For example, the supplied illustration shows:

- **323 soldiers:** 4:51:10 hours of healing
- **30 soldiers:** 0:27:03 hours of healing
- with **5 helpers:** approximately 37 minutes of possible reduction as the illustrated guideline

The older illustration’s approximately 37-minute value belongs to the buffs used at that time. With the now documented Help Time of 742 seconds, five helpers instead reduce up to **1:01:50**. This shows why neither 20–40 soldiers nor a blanket 30–50-minute timer can be optimal for every profile.

## Determine your own optimal batch size

The best size is the **largest batch whose healing time is fully covered by the alliance helps expected immediately**.

### Simple testing procedure

1. Read your personal Help Time.
2. Count members from whom you can expect immediate help.
3. Multiply the two values and convert the result into hours, minutes and seconds.
4. Select enough soldiers for the healing timer to fall just below that result.
5. Request help and check whether every planned member responds.
6. Adjust the next batch:
   - all helps arrive immediately and the batch ends → keep the target time,
   - fewer helps than expected → reduce the next batch accordingly,
   - additional helpers reliably become active → recalculate the next batch.

This produces a personal guideline better than a fixed soldier count.

### When fewer members are online

With few helpers, the batch must be smaller if it should still finish instantly. Count only members likely to actually respond. Five players shown online do not automatically equal five immediate helps.

Possible responses:

- use smaller batches,
- briefly ask for help in alliance chat,
- postpone healing to a more active period if hospital capacity allows,
- or deliberately start a longer batch if you will not keep fighting anyway.

## Why healing the entire hospital at once is often unfavorable

One large healing task is convenient, but makes poor use of repeated alliance help while you are online:

- The timer may last several hours.
- Available helps may not reduce it to zero.
- You cannot start another healing batch while the remaining time runs.
- The alliance can help you again only on the next task.
- Speedups or diamonds may then be needed for an immediate return to battle.

Small batches instead create many short healing tasks. Each task can receive help again.

## When a large healing task is still right

The batch method requires starting the next task immediately after completion. If you go to sleep, work or stop checking the game for a longer period, a small healing task would finish quickly and leave the hospital idle afterward.

Before going offline, therefore select enough soldiers for healing time to roughly match your planned absence.

| Situation | Recommended approach |
| --- | --- |
| Many members online, you remain active | small batches, request help immediately, repeat directly |
| Few helpers online | smaller test batches or accept longer healing |
| You are going offline briefly | set a batch lasting roughly until your return |
| You are going offline overnight | start a large healing task lasting several hours |
| Fighting continues and the hospital fills again | prioritize short batches and actively coordinate help |

> **Offline rule:** Do not select the smallest possible quantity; keep the queue usefully occupied throughout your absence.

## Coordinate alliance help properly

The method works only if other members actually press “Help”. Good organization therefore increases its effect:

- Heal during periods of high alliance activity where possible.
- Announce a shared healing phase in chat after major battles.
- Consistently help other members too, so everyone can finish batches quickly.
- Start the next batch immediately while helpers remain online.
- Do not use speedups before the available helps arrive.

Alliance research can improve personal Help Time. In the example, **94 of the total 742 seconds** come from Alliance Tech. An active alliance therefore benefits twice: many members provide helps, and stronger technology increases the time reduction per help.

## Incorporate healing bonuses sensibly

Healing speed and hospital capacity change your personal ideal value. In particular, the Tactical Minister from Tip 3 offers, according to the available role overview:

- **+20% healing speed**
- **+20% hospital capacity**

If you receive a temporary healing bonus, recheck the timer and adjust batch size. A bonus may let you include more soldiers in a batch with the same target duration.

Whether a bonus applies only to healing started after activation should be checked against the current office or bonus description. Do not apply rules from other action types to healing without verification.

## Resources, speedups and “free healing”

Community guides sometimes speak of “free” or “instant” healing. They mean that alliance help can finish a short batch without **diamonds or speedups**.

This does not automatically mean normal healing resources are unnecessary. Check hospital resource requirements before each start. The method primarily reduces **time** and saves optional acceleration items.

## A sensible procedure after major battles

1. Check hospital occupancy and remaining capacity.
2. Read personal Help Time and Help Limit in the buff overview.
3. Determine in alliance chat how many helpers can respond immediately.
4. Calculate Help Time × expected helpers.
5. Set soldier count so the timer falls just below the calculated instant-heal time.
6. Request help and check that the expected reduction occurs.
7. Repeat without idle time while online.
8. Switch to a long batch before going offline.
9. After returning, recheck remaining wounded soldiers and hospital capacity.

## Common mistakes

- **Healing the entire hospital at once:** This prevents efficient repeated help.
- **Using only soldier count:** Forty high-tier soldiers may have a different healing time from forty lower-tier soldiers.
- **Confusing Help Limit with available helpers:** A limit of 25 does not guarantee 25 members are online and will help immediately.
- **Counting every online player:** Count only members likely to press “Help” in time.
- **Failing to recheck Help Time:** Survivors, buildings and Alliance Tech can change your personal value.
- **Requesting alliance help too late:** The earlier you request, the earlier members can respond.
- **Not continuing after one batch:** The method gains its speed through repetition.
- **Choosing overly large batches when activity is low:** Without sufficient helpers, a long remaining timer persists.
- **Starting a tiny batch before going offline:** It ends quickly and leaves the healing queue empty for the rest of the absence.
- **Using speedups immediately:** First wait to see how far alliance help reduces the timer.
- **Confusing “free” with resource-free:** Alliance help mainly saves time, diamonds and speedups; normal healing costs may still apply.
- **Ignoring hospital capacity:** During longer battles, regularly check how much space remains.

## Limitations and information still to verify

- The current screenshot shows Help Time 742 and Help Limit 25. These values apply to the shown profile, not automatically to other members.
- The calculation assumes the full Help Time value in seconds is subtracted from the timer. Cross-check the example with a real healing batch and five helps.
- The older illustration’s approximately 37 minutes for five helpers reflects different buffs and is not universally replaced by this profile’s current value.
- 20–40 soldiers or 30–50 minutes remain only general starting ranges; personal calculation using Help Time and reliably available helpers is more precise.
- The effect of soldier tier and wound type on healing time per unit is not fully documented.
- Check the interaction between Tactical Minister, personal research, alliance research and other healing bonuses against the current timer.
- Hospital capacity, available helpers and the active battle situation may require different priorities from maximum time efficiency.
