# 💡 Tip #4 – Teleport correctly

## Key points

- **Random Relocator:** inexpensive, but places your base at a random position on the map’s edge.
- **Alliance Relocator:** moves your base close to the alliance and is usually the best choice for regrouping quickly.
- **Advanced Relocator:** allows a specific position, but costs three times as much at 7,500 alliance coins.
- **Independent items:** The three relocators are separate teleport items, not successive upgrade tiers.
- **Do not react too late:** You cannot teleport during a battle. Withdraw in time.

> **Remember:** Random for flexibility, Alliance for regrouping, Advanced for an exact position.

![Tip 4 – Teleport correctly](../../../../media/knowledge/Tipps/Tipp_04_RichtigTeleportieren.png)

## What is this tip for?

This tip helps you choose the right relocator. All three items move your base, but differ significantly in **where** you arrive and how many alliance coins their use costs.

Used correctly, relocators help you:

- return quickly to the alliance,
- shorten marches to shared targets,
- take an agreed position in the alliance formation,
- leave dangerous areas in time, and
- use expensive Advanced Relocators only when an exact position is really necessary.

## Direct comparison of the three relocators

| Relocator | Destination | Price in the alliance shop | Typical use |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| ❓ **Random Relocator** | random location on the map’s edge | 2,500 | quick, inexpensive relocation without needing a particular position |
| 🚩 **Alliance Relocator** | game-selected position near the alliance | 2,500 | returning to the alliance, fighting together or providing support |
| 📍 **Advanced Relocator** | specifically chosen available position | 7,500 | precise placement, coordinated formation or strategic location |

One Advanced Relocator therefore costs as much as **three Random or three Alliance Relocators**. The higher price buys control over the exact destination, rather than a more powerful teleport effect.

## What “independent” means

The three relocators are different items, each with its own purpose. You do not need to use a Random Relocator followed by an Alliance Relocator before being able to use an Advanced Relocator.

Choose the item that directly suits your goal. An unnecessary intermediate teleport only costs another item and may leave you in a worse position.

## Random Relocator

The Random Relocator is inexpensive but gives you no control over the destination. Your base is placed at a **random position on the map’s edge**. It is suitable when changing location quickly matters more than the exact position and you deliberately want to arrive outside the central map areas.

### Useful situations

- You want to leave an area without needing to reach specific coordinates.
- You want to change position without using an expensive Advanced Relocator.
- The new position is only a temporary location.

### Limitations

- The random edge position may be far from the alliance and important map objects.
- The new location is not automatically safe or strategically useful.
- A second teleport may be necessary if the result is unsuitable.

> **Decision aid:** If you want to join the alliance afterward anyway, the equally priced Alliance Relocator is usually the more sensible choice.

## Alliance Relocator

The Alliance Relocator takes you close to the alliance. It is the standard choice when you are away from the group and want to play alongside other members again.

### Useful situations

- You are far from the alliance after an event or relocation.
- The alliance is gathering for a joint attack or defense.
- You want shorter marches for support and shared targets.
- You want to withdraw from a dangerous area toward your own group.

### Limitations

“Near the alliance” does not automatically mean your preferred exact spot. The game determines the destination according to its placement rules and available space.

If a formation requires a particular gap, row or coordinate, the Advanced Relocator is the more reliable choice.

## Advanced Relocator

The Advanced Relocator lets you freely select a particular available position. It is therefore the most precise, but also the most expensive, of the three items.

### Useful situations

- You are expected to occupy previously agreed coordinates.
- The alliance is building an orderly formation around a target or strong member.
- You deliberately want a position with short marches to an event or combat area.
- An Alliance Relocator would take you to the right area but not reliably to the required spot.

### Check before confirming

- Is the selected space actually free?
- Is the position within the agreed alliance formation?
- Are opponents or other dangers immediately nearby?
- Can important targets be reached by short routes from there?
- Is the exact position worth the additional 5,000 alliance coins compared with the Alliance Relocator?

## Which relocator should I choose?

| Goal | Recommended relocator | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Simply leave the current location | **Random Relocator** | inexpensive if the destination does not matter |
| Return to your alliance | **Alliance Relocator** | same price as Random, but aimed toward the right area |
| Fight together or provide support | **Alliance Relocator** | generally shortens routes to the group |
| Occupy exact coordinates | **Advanced Relocator** | only this relocator provides specific position control |
| Close a gap in the alliance formation | **Advanced Relocator** | precise placement matters more than the price |
| Escape during an ongoing battle | **None** | teleporting is blocked during battle |

## Safe procedure before teleporting

1. **Define your purpose:** Do you want to escape, return to the alliance or reach an exact position?
2. **Choose a suitable item:** Do not automatically use the most expensive relocator.
3. **Check combat status:** If your base is already in battle, it is too late to teleport.
4. **Check the destination area:** For a selectable position, review the surroundings, opponents, available space and alliance agreements.
5. **Check the item and cost:** Before confirming, ensure the intended relocator is actually selected.
6. **Teleport:** Confirm only once the position and purpose match.
7. **Check after teleporting:** Review your new location and adjust further marches or defensive measures accordingly.

## Withdraw from dangerous areas in time

The most important safety point is the combat restriction. A relocator is a tool for **preparation and timely reaction**, not escape from a battle already underway.

When danger approaches:

1. Check the situation and the opponent’s distance.
2. Decide whether defense, support or withdrawal makes more sense.
3. Select the required relocator.
4. Complete the teleport before the battle begins.

> **Important:** You cannot teleport during a battle. Waiting until impact removes your safe withdrawal option.

The available in-game illustration does not explain which additional states “in battle” includes. Whether active marches, rally participation or particular combat-status indicators also block it should be tested and documented separately.

## Spend alliance coins wisely

Alliance coins accumulate over time and are also needed for other scarce items. A community recommendation is therefore to save them primarily for peace shields, teleports and talent resets rather than easily obtained basic resources.

For relocators, this means:

- Keep at least one suitable emergency teleport ready instead of needing to shop while in danger.
- Use the cheaper Alliance Relocator for ordinary returns to the alliance where possible.
- Use the Advanced Relocator only when exact coordinates provide a real tactical advantage.
- Do not spend your entire coin balance if combat or relocation phases are approaching.

A suitable reserve depends on activity, alliance plans and shop stock. No universally applicable minimum number is publicly established.

## Common mistakes

- **Confusing random relocation with safety:** Random means neither close to the alliance nor free of enemies.
- **Using the Alliance Relocator for exact placement:** It takes you nearby but does not guarantee the desired coordinates.
- **Wasting an Advanced Relocator:** Simply returning to the alliance often costs 2,500 rather than 7,500 coins.
- **Waiting until battle:** Once fighting starts, teleporting is no longer possible.
- **Teleporting without coordination:** A freely chosen position can disrupt a planned formation or reserved gap.
- **Planning without a reserve:** An empty inventory usually becomes apparent precisely when rapid relocation is needed.

## Limitations and information still to verify

- Effects, prices and the combat restriction are documented by the supplied in-game illustration, but may change with updates or shop adjustments.
- It remains unclear exactly how the game determines proximity for the Alliance Relocator’s destination.
- Testing is still needed to establish which active states, besides an ongoing battle, block use.
- Shop stock, purchase limits and refresh timing are not documented in the available material.
- It is not established whether a failed placement attempt consumes an item; always check the confirmation dialog.
- Different teleport rules may apply in cross-server areas or special events. Rely on the current in-game display there.
