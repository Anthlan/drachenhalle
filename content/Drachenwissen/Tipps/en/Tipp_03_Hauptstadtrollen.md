# 💡 Tip #3 – Capital roles

## Key points

- **Choose the right role:** Construction, research, training, healing and resources each have their own capital role.
- **Title first, action second:** Construction, research, training or healing already underway does not receive the bonus retroactively.
- **Prepare major actions:** Have resources, prerequisites and an available queue ready before receiving the title.
- **Check activation:** Before starting, verify that the title is actually active and that the displayed duration or capacity has decreased or increased.
- **Vice President:** This title is available only to members of a top-three alliance.

> **Remember:** Get appointed first, check the bonus, then start the major action.

![Tip 3 – Special capital roles](../../../../media/knowledge/Tipps/Tipp_03_Hauptstadtrollen.png)

## What is this tip for?

Capital roles give their holders powerful but temporary bonuses. Used correctly, they can shorten lengthy construction and research, enable larger training batches, improve healing after heavy battles or increase resource production.

The decisive factor is **timing**: a title does not apply retroactively to an action already underway. Prepare everything before appointment so that you can start the desired action immediately after receiving the title.

## Overview of capital roles

| Capital role | Listed bonuses | Particularly suitable for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🏗️ **Minister of Construction** | +50% construction speed · +25% research speed | long building or base upgrades; alternatively, major research |
| 🔬 **Research Director** | +50% research speed · +25% construction speed | lengthy technologies; alternatively, a major building upgrade |
| ⚔️ **Minister of Defense** | +20% training speed · +20% training limit | large soldier-training batches, preparation for combat or scoring events |
| 🏥 **Tactical Minister** | +20% healing speed · +20% hospital capacity | healing after major battles and before further fighting |
| 🌾 **Civil Minister** | +100% food, metal and oil | periods of high resource production or collection |

The table reproduces values documented in the alliance illustration. Game versions and translations may differ, so the current in-game role display always takes priority.

## The most important rule: Start time determines the bonus

Based on observations so far, the following applies to construction, research, training and healing:

| Order | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Start action → receive title | **No retroactive bonus** for the action already underway |
| Receive title → start action | **Bonus is applied when starting** |

### No bonus for actions already underway

If construction is already underway and you become Minister of Construction afterward, the remaining construction time is not automatically recalculated. The same applies to research, training and healing that have already started.

### Start the action during the term of office

Get appointed first, then check the displayed duration or capacity. Only start the prepared action once the title is visibly active.

A community source reports that an action started during the term retains its reduced duration even if the title expires before completion. This fits the observed start-time logic, but the available in-game illustration does not explicitly prove it. When possible, check the timer before and after a term ends and document the result.

## Preparation before appointment

A powerful title offers little benefit if you still need to find resources, meet prerequisites or free a queue during the brief term. Prepare the following:

1. **Choose a suitable action:** Decide in advance which building, research project, training batch or healing action to start.
2. **Check prerequisites:** Required building levels, research or available hospital space must already be in place.
3. **Prepare resources:** Open resource crates only as necessary and ensure all costs are covered.
4. **Keep a queue free:** The construction, research, training or healing slot must be available at appointment time.
5. **Coordinate with leadership:** State which role you need and when you will be ready to start.
6. **Check the title:** After appointment, open the role or profile display and confirm that the correct title is active.
7. **Compare the preview:** Check the displayed duration, production amount or capacity before the final start.
8. **Start the action:** Only now confirm the prepared action.

> **Practical rule:** Do not start planning after appointment. The term is for starting, not searching.

## Which role suits which action?

### Minister of Construction

The Minister of Construction is the first choice for a particularly long or expensive building upgrade. It is especially valuable for projects lasting several days or blocking further base development.

If no major construction project is currently possible, the additional research bonus may still be useful. For genuinely lengthy research, however, the Research Director is the stronger role.

### Research Director

Use the Research Director for lengthy technologies. Prepare the research fully and ensure that no other technology blocks the queue.

The additional construction bonus is a useful alternative, but it does not match the Minister of Construction’s main bonus.

### Minister of Defense

This role combines faster training with a higher training limit. It therefore offers the greatest benefit with a large prepared training batch.

Before starting, check both the displayed soldier count and training duration. This shows whether both parts of the bonus are active. The role is particularly useful before combat phases or on event days when training earns points.

### Tactical Minister

The Tactical Minister improves healing speed and hospital capacity. Preferably use it after heavy battles or immediately before another planned combat phase.

Exercise caution with the additional hospital capacity: until reproducible testing establishes how the game handles capacity dropping again after the term ends, do not deliberately fill the hospital far beyond its normal capacity.

### Civil Minister

The Civil Minister increases food, metal and oil production. Unlike construction, research, training and healing, this is not clearly a queue action started once.

Check in the game:

- whether the bonus produces resources only during the active term,
- whether collection time matters, and
- how it combines with existing production bonuses.

The available illustration confirms the +100% value, but not the exact calculation logic. Until a reproducible test is available, do not present any further claim as an established rule.

## Read speed bonuses correctly

A **+50% speed** bonus does not automatically mean **50% less time**. With a simple additive calculation, an action with 100 units of base speed would run at 150 units afterward. Its duration would be approximately two-thirds of the original time – about a 33% saving.

Existing bonuses from research, VIP, equipment or events can further affect the additional saving. The preview displayed in the game is therefore more reliable than a blanket conversion.

### Simplified calculation example

Assuming a simple additive calculation with no other bonuses:

| Speed bonus | Remaining duration | Approximate time saved |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| +20% | 83.3% | 16.7% |
| +25% | 80.0% | 20.0% |
| +50% | 66.7% | 33.3% |

This table is mathematical guidance, not a confirmed game formula. Always use the in-game time display for actual planning.

## Vice President and top-three alliances

According to the documented in-game notice, the **Vice President** title is available exclusively to members of the three highest-ranked alliances.

This tip does not list a specific Vice President bonus. No additional effect is therefore inferred from the eligibility requirement. A screenshot of the role description and its displayed values is still needed for complete documentation.

## Recommended alliance procedure

A clear process helps as many members as possible benefit:

1. Members register the role and planned action they need.
2. Only fully prepared players enter the current appointment round.
3. A responsible person assigns the roles in sequence.
4. Each recipient briefly confirms that the title is visible.
5. The prepared action is started immediately.
6. A successful start is confirmed so that the role can be passed on.

A waiting list or fixed appointment times help with particularly popular roles. This avoids wasting a term while a recipient still searches for resources or prerequisites.

## Common mistakes

- **Starting too early:** An action already underway does not receive the bonus retroactively.
- **Requesting the wrong role:** A +25% secondary bonus is weaker than the matching +50% main bonus.
- **Blocking the queue:** The prepared action cannot start during the appointment.
- **Failing to check the title:** The action is triggered before the appointment is actually active.
- **Misunderstanding percentages:** A speed bonus is confused with an identical reduction in time.
- **Adopting an untested special rule:** Community claims are used without comparison against the current in-game display.
