# 💡 Tip #15 – Small tricks, big impact

## Key points

- **Shake off an attacking Watcher:** Start a rally, wait about 30 seconds, then cancel it. According to current DIE experience, the Watcher forgets its target and turns away.
- **Secure Special Ops:** A mission can only be pillaged after completion. Claim it immediately when it finishes.
- **Check monster losses:** Even successful monster attacks can kill soldiers. Review battle reports regularly.
- **Recognize “ghosts”:** A rally join with unusually low power is currently observed as an automatic system join; the player is offline.
- **Share directly:** Battle reports and coordinates can be shared straight into group chats, which is faster and clearer than a screenshot.

> **Rule of thumb:** Distract Watchers, claim missions immediately, read losses, recognize ghosts and share information directly.

![Tip 15 – Small tricks, big impact](../../../../media/knowledge/Tipps/Tipp_15_KleineTricksGrosseWirkung.png)

## What is this tip for?

This collection records small mechanics that are easy to miss but can save time, soldiers and unnecessary questions. The five items do not belong to one event; they are grouped because each can be used immediately without lengthy preparation.

## 1. Shake off an attacking Watcher

According to current DIE experience, you can make a Watcher abandon an attack on your base:

1. Start a rally.
2. Keep it active for about **30 seconds**.
3. Cancel the rally.
4. Check the world map and confirm that the Watcher has turned away.

The rally does not need to launch. The 30 seconds are an observed value, not an officially documented timer. Always verify the Watcher’s movement on the map.

This small defensive trick does not replace a dedicated Watcher guide. Team strength, losses, participant count and resource management will be covered separately.

## 2. Claim Special Ops immediately

A Special Ops mission may be watched before it ends, but it can only be pillaged **after completion**. For valuable missions, check the remaining time, remember the finish time or set a timer, and claim the reward immediately after completion.

A visible observer does not mean the mission has already been pillaged; another player may simply be waiting for completion.

## 3. Check dead soldiers after monster attacks

Monster battles can kill soldiers even when the fight is won. Open battle reports regularly and distinguish deployed, surviving, wounded and dead soldiers.

### DIE example

A total loss of **1,700 soldiers** against a Watcher resulted in approximately **170 dead soldiers**.

```text
170 dead ÷ 1,700 total loss = 10%
```

Ten percent describes this example only and is not a universal death rate. Monster level, teams, participant count and game mode can change the result. Tip #12 explains a full battle-report analysis.

## 4. Recognize automatic joins as “ghosts”

When a team with **unusually low power** joins a rally, our current observation identifies it as an automatic system join. The associated player is offline and did not join manually.

Anthlan calls these automatic offline participants **“ghosts.”** For regular DIE players, we expect teams of at least about 500,000 power. This is not a fixed system threshold but a reference value: what matters is that the power is conspicuously below the usual level of active members.

## 5. Share battle reports and coordinates directly

Battle reports and coordinates can be shared directly into a group chat. Coordinates remain usable, reports are easier to find and questions are clearer than with a screenshot.

Open the report or coordinate, use the share action and select the intended chat group. Add a short question to battle reports so others immediately know what to check. Tip #12 helps with the joint analysis.

## Limitations and open questions

- The 30-second rally cancellation and Watcher target reset are current DIE observations and are not documented in a public official rules page.
- We have not separately documented whether every rally target triggers the same Watcher behavior.
- The 1,700-to-170 loss ratio is an example, not a formula.
- “Unusually low” is a relative indicator, not a fixed system threshold; the alliance’s usual power level may continue to develop.
- The location and wording of the share action may change with interface updates.

## Sources and reliability

- **DIE practice:** The Watcher reset, example losses and “ghosts” recognized by unusually low power come from current alliance observations.
- **Special Ops:** A detailed [community guide](https://rozamira74.ru/z-route-redemption-gajd-polnoe-rukovodstvo-dlya-novichkov-2026/) describes players watching missions before completion and pillaging after they finish.
- **Watcher losses:** [Z Route Academy – Attacking Watchers](https://zroute.dev/es/combat/attacking-watchers/) documents troop losses from firsthand Watcher battles and marks loss figures as approximate.
- **Direct sharing and changing offline rules:** [Z Route Academy – Updates](https://zroute.dev/updates/) lists group-chat sharing for reports and coordinates as well as later changes to offline-join behavior.
