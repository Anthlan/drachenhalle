In **Alliance Ambush**, coordinated action matters: start five rallies of your own for your personal quests and join at least five rallies started by other members. Additional joins help the entire alliance.

Smaller teams are often sufficient at easier levels, avoiding unnecessary soldier losses. If one member reaches the damage required for **MVP x10**, all participants receive the x10 bonus.
