From **21:00**, several zombie waves attack our bases one after another. Set your defence in time and hold out together for as long as possible.

- **Shields do not protect you in this event.**
- Losses go to the **hospital**; soldiers do not die.
- If your defence is breached, your event ends.
- The more waves we successfully defend individually and as an alliance, the better the rewards.

**Do not want to participate?** Leave your defence empty; your event then ends after the first wave.

**Need support?** Let us know before the start so we can help defend. If you have questions, message Anthlan or an R4.
