Whether our server attacks or defends is only decided on Friday by the war-zone points accumulated during the week. Follow the current deployment plan and your assigned role.

Attackers must capture the enemy capital; defenders hold it until the end. The combat phase lasts no more than four hours and can end earlier once either side reaches **100 % progress**.

Coordination is crucial: strong players lead rallies or hold the capital and batteries. Supporters fill rallies, reinforce assigned targets and secure important positions. Remember that offensive actions remove your shield and trigger **15 minutes of Battle Frenzy**.

More information: [Expedition Frenzy – detailed event guide](https://zroutegame.com/expedition-frenzy/)
