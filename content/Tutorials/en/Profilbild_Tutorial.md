# From gallery avatar to in-game profile image

## Check the requirements

**Before starting, check whether uploads are available for your account.** The feature unlocks when either condition is met:

<img class="tutorial-side-character" src="/drachenhalle/tutorial/profilbild/12_somea_pruefung_chibi.webp" alt="Somea explains the avatar upload requirements with an hourglass and check mark." decoding="async">

- Your base has reached at least **level 25**.
- You have made at least **one real-money purchase**.

Whichever happens first counts. These restrictions also apply:

- Only **one custom avatar** can be saved per account.
- You can submit only **one image per hour** for review.
- If the filter rejects an image, you still have to wait one hour before trying again.
- The game lists general prohibitions, but the filter's exact review criteria are unknown.

> **Important:** Choose your image and crop carefully. An unsuccessful review attempt also triggers the one-hour wait.

> **Shortcut:** If you already have a suitable image in your photo album, go straight to [Step 5: Open your profile](#open-your-profile). If you still need an image in the alliance style, feel free to contact **Anthlan**. A separate guide to creating one with ChatGPT will follow later.

## Open the gallery

**Open the Dragon Hall and use the menu to go to the gallery.** On a smartphone, the menu button is at the top right, followed by the **“Gallery”** entry.

<img class="tutorial-process-shot is-phone-composite" src="/drachenhalle/tutorial/profilbild/16_galerie_im_smartphone.webp" alt="Anthlan shows the highlighted Gallery menu entry on a smartphone" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

[Open the gallery directly](/drachenhalle/en/galerie/?kategorie=Avatar)

## Choose an avatar

**Enable the “Avatars” category in the gallery and select your character.** Under **“Style”**, narrow the selection further if needed.

Tap the button with the **down arrow** on your chosen avatar. Download the original rather than taking a screenshot of the gallery view.

<img class="tutorial-process-shot is-phone-composite" src="/drachenhalle/tutorial/profilbild/17_avatarwahl_im_smartphone.webp" alt="Anthlan shows highlighted avatar filters and the marked download button on the left avatar image on a smartphone" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

## Save the original on your device

**After tapping the download icon, your device's sharing or saving view opens.** The illustration shows **iOS**. Select **“Save Image”**. On Android, the view and wording may differ depending on device and browser.

<img class="tutorial-process-shot is-wide" src="/drachenhalle/tutorial/profilbild/03_bild_sichern_markiert.webp" alt="iPhone selection view with Save Image clearly marked" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

The original is then in your photo or image gallery and can be selected in the game.

## Open your profile

**Return to the game and open your profile using your avatar image.** Then tap the small editing pencil on the profile image.

<img class="tutorial-process-shot is-phone-composite" src="/drachenhalle/tutorial/profilbild/18_profil_im_smartphone_id_geschuetzt.webp" alt="Anthlan shows a suitably cropped profile on a smartphone with the avatar editing pencil marked; the player ID is completely concealed" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

This opens **“Change Avatar”**.

## Prepare the custom slot

**Only one custom avatar can be saved in the game at a time.** An existing custom image is identified by the small red delete button.

<img class="tutorial-process-shot is-comparison" src="/drachenhalle/tutorial/profilbild/14_custom_avatar_workflow_v3.webp" alt="Two-part Dragon Hall guide: delete the existing custom avatar using the correctly marked red button, then tap the marked camera icon; the bottom Use buttons are fully visible" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

Then tap the **camera icon** to upload a new custom avatar.

## Start the upload

**Read the game's notice carefully and select “View Album”.** The game also explains that the avatar is reviewed and no further upload is possible during review.

<img class="tutorial-process-shot is-phone-composite" src="/drachenhalle/tutorial/profilbild/19_uploadhinweis_im_smartphone_v2.webp" alt="Anthlan shows the cropped upload notice on a smartphone with fully visible buttons and a marker centred over View Album" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

The listed content rules are binding. As the automatic filter's exact operation is unknown, choose an image that is clearly unproblematic.

> **Remember the wait:** Even if the image is rejected, uploads are still blocked for one hour.

## Set the crop

**Select the avatar previously saved in your album and adjust the crop.** Enlarge and move the image so the face, horns and important details stay inside the white frame.

<img class="tutorial-process-shot is-comparison" src="/drachenhalle/tutorial/profilbild/15_bildausschnitt_workflow.webp" alt="Two-part Dragon Hall guide: first choose the crop, then enlarge the image appropriately" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

Tap **“Confirm”** at the top right when the crop fits.

## Submit and wait for review

**Check the result again in the avatar preview.** Clicking **“Confirm”** submits the avatar for review.

<img class="tutorial-process-shot is-phone-composite" src="/drachenhalle/tutorial/profilbild/20_einreichen_im_smartphone.webp" alt="Anthlan presents the full avatar preview on a smartphone with the Confirm button clearly visible and marked" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

Review may take up to **one hour**. You cannot upload another image during this time. After successful review, the avatar appears in your profile. If rejected, the one-hour upload block remains and you can try again only afterwards.

> **Done:** Your gallery avatar is cropped and submitted for review. Check your profile later to see the result.
