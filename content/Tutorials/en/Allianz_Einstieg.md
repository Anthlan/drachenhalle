## Who we are

**DIE is a community built on clear agreements and acting together.** We want to grow together with commitment and enjoyment. Rules protect the alliance from unnecessary conflict, defined areas of responsibility for our officers (R4) make help easy to find, and Dragon Knowledge records experience for everyone.

![Anthlan, mysteryZ, DaVinci, Somea and Drachenherz build the Dragon Hall together and plant a young tree](/drachenhalle/tutorial/03_gemeinsam_wachsen_chibi.webp)

What you can expect from us:

- We coordinate shared events and battles.
- We support each other with alliance help and shared knowledge.
- The leader (R5) and officers (R4) coordinate broader issues together and cover for one another.
- Community, combat, research, recruitment and the freight train have clearly assigned responsibilities.

You do not have to work out who handles a topic by yourself. [Our officers](../../Allianz/en/Allianz_Offiziere.md) shows the relevant area. If you are unsure, you can always ask the leader (R5) or an available officer (R4).

> **What we stand for:** Growing together with commitment and enjoyment.

## Our rules

**Know these four rules before your first combat or raiding action:**

<img class="tutorial-side-character" src="/drachenhalle/tutorial/04_regeln_mysteryz.webp" alt="mysteryZ presents a shield and a checked rule list." loading="lazy" decoding="async">

1. **Respect NAP10:** Do not attack members of a current NAP10 alliance. Recheck the rankings and alliance membership before every attack.
2. **Attack permitted targets proportionately:** Players without an alliance and players named **Commander\*\*\*\*** may be attacked, but must not be reduced completely to **0**.
3. **Raid only on other servers:** Do not raid trucks, freight trains or special missions on our own server. Check the server number before starting.
4. **Do not retaliate after a NAP10 attack:** Share the attack report and report the attacker's name, alliance and the time to an officer (R4) or the leader (R5). Leadership will handle the resolution.

If unsure, always **check or ask first, then act**. A first rule violation receives a warning; a second warning leads to removal.

[Read all alliance rules with examples](../../Allianz/en/Allianz_Regeln.md)

> **Remember:** Check rankings and server. Report a NAP10 incident rather than responding yourself.

## Our netiquette

**Good communication makes coordination easier.** Be friendly, join in and help keep our time together relaxed.

<img class="tutorial-side-character" src="/drachenhalle/tutorial/05_netiquette_somea.webp" alt="Somea invites conversation with a friendly gesture." loading="lazy" decoding="async">

- Treat others respectfully and avoid topics generally considered taboo.
- Feel welcome to join conversations and shared activities without pressuring others.
- We enjoy capturing special moments or illustrating ideas with ChatGPT-generated images.
- Ask questions early. If the responsible role is unclear, the leader (R5) or any available officer (R4) can help.
- When reporting an attack, include **the attacker's name, alliance and the time** where possible, and share the attack report.
- Discuss concerns, problems and fairness with the **Goddess** or alliance leadership; confidential issues should not be discussed in public alliance chat.
- During shared activities, follow current guidance from event or combat coordinators.
- Please let us know beforehand about holidays or longer inactivity. Members who do not notify us are usually removed after **seven days of inactivity**.

[Meet our officers and learn their areas of responsibility](../../Allianz/en/Allianz_Offiziere.md)

> **In brief:** Be friendly, think ahead and let us know if you will be away for longer.

## Important game settings

**Exclude targets on our own server when raiding.** In the **“Other players' transport trucks”** view, tick **“Exclude transport trucks from this server”**.

![Enabled checkbox: “Exclude transport trucks from this server”](/drachenhalle/tutorial/01_transportlastwagen_server_ausschliessen.webp)

This means only trucks from other servers are offered for raiding. It supports our rule against raiding trucks on our own server. Still check the server again before every action.

### Special missions and containers

We also raid special missions – containers on the map – **only on other servers**. Before starting, check the display at the top of the screen: on another server it may say **“War Zone S107”**. No server indicator appears on our own server, **105**. If it is missing, do not raid the container; move to another server first.

When special missions are shared in chat, identify the server from the first number in the coordinates. **`[S:105 …]` is our own server and therefore off limits.** In **`[S:107 X:686 Y:467]`**, the mission is on a permitted foreign server.

## Your first steps

**This short checklist guides your start without overwhelming you.**

![Anthlan and DaVinci guide a young new member through choosing a name, the rally point, shield supplies and research donations](/drachenhalle/tutorial/02_erste_schritte_chibi.webp)

### In the first 24 hours

- [ ] Read the [complete alliance rules](../../Allianz/en/Allianz_Regeln.md).
- [ ] Choose your own player name – please do not keep a name like **Commander\*\*\*\***.
- [ ] Move to the **alliance gathering point**, unless otherwise agreed with leadership.
- [ ] Enable **“Exclude transport trucks from this server”**.
- [ ] Keep at least one **24-hour shield** and one **8-hour shield** in your inventory.
- [ ] Open [Our officers](../../Allianz/en/Allianz_Offiziere.md) and note your contacts.
- [ ] Check the next [events](/drachenhalle/en/termine/) and their guidance.
- [ ] Check current rankings before attacks and the server number before raids.

### In your first week

- [ ] If you want your own profile image, follow the [profile image tutorial](Profilbild_Tutorial.md).
- [ ] Browse the [tips and gameplay knowledge](/drachenhalle/en/docs/tipps/).
- [ ] Read the [rallies tip](../../Drachenwissen/Tipps/en/Tipp_02_Sammelangriffe.md) before supporting shared attacks.
- [ ] During events, follow current coordination in alliance chat.
- [ ] Give alliance help when fellow players request support.
- [ ] Donate regularly towards prioritised alliance research – everyone benefits.

## Working together in the alliance

**Many actions are faster and more effective together.** The key is following current guidance and selecting the right team for the shared action.

<img class="tutorial-side-character" src="/drachenhalle/tutorial/06_zusammenspiel_drachenherz.webp" alt="Drachenherz invites cooperation with the alliance banner." loading="lazy" decoding="async">

### How you can help directly

- **Give alliance help:** Among other things, help reduces ongoing healing time. Fast responses benefit everyone, especially after battles.
- **Support rallies thoughtfully:** Put strong teams first, observe the order and follow combat coordination.
- **Prepare for events:** Check the date, description and current guidance before the action begins.
- **Focus research:** Donate to the node prioritised by the research director using the leadership star.
- **Ask the right contacts:** The Warlord and ambush coordinator handle battles and Alliance Ambush; the train coordinator organises the freight train.
