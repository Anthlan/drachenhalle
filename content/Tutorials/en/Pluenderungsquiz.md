# Raiding quiz

Which targets may we raid? Practise our rules in six short scenarios. Each answer comes with an explanation, followed by your result at the end. You can repeat the quiz as often as you like.

This covers **trucks, freight trains and special missions**. Our own server is **S105**; we raid only on other servers.
