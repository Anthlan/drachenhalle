# Thor63 – The rune-bound guardian

## Scope

This profile describes only Thor63's fictional gaming alter ego in the Dragon Knights' visual world. It draws no conclusions about the real person's personal qualities.

## Source material

The basis consists of two current avatars, the existing character model and guidance confirmed during its development about demeanour, apparent age, posture, clothing, blind eye and preferred mood.

## Core character

Thor63 is an old, dignified guardian who does not seek conflict and therefore has no need to prove his strength. He observes at length, rarely speaks first and shares a clear opinion when asked directly. His calm is not coldness. It comes from self-respect, experience and choosing not to answer every disturbance with force.

He appreciates fine, special things without showing off. Fabrics, metal and personal possessions may be high-quality but appear aged, selected and long-worn. He needs no splendour to seem imposing.

## Recurring qualities

- He is quiet and rarely speaks first.
- When addressed directly, he responds attentively with a clear opinion of his own.
- He seems stoic but not unfriendly.
- He has great self-respect and avoids unnecessary conflict.
- Age and experience give him weight without making him frail.
- He values quality, durability and distinctive craftsmanship.
- His posture remains contained and controlled; weapons are not presented challengingly.
- Different looks and small expression changes tell more than grand gestures.

## Relationships and role in the ensemble

Thor63 belongs to leadership without defining himself through volume or visible claims. He is more the voice answering a direct question with a considered judgement than the person opening every discussion. His influence comes from the reliability of his position and calm brought to charged situations.

## Visual identity

- old man with strong, venerable build
- distinctive, deeply lined face
- thick white-grey beard and strong white eyebrows
- anatomically right eye blind and milky; anatomically left eye clear and ice blue
- deep midnight-blue hood shading forehead and parts of the face
- heavy multilayered robe and cloak of coarse, high-quality wool
- restrained Nordic edging in aged bronze or silver
- massive plain rune hammer, usually lowered or used as support
- midnight blue, charcoal, wet stone, aged silver, dark leather and little warm lantern light
- visible fabric fibres, wear, rain, patina and irregular edges rather than clean glossy surfaces

## His own world

### The hall of quiet thunder

High above a wet rocky pass, an old Nordic hall stands between dark pillars and deep archways. Thunderstorms cross the mountains outside; inside remain only a few lanterns and low hearth embers. Thunder is audible, but the hall does not answer with noise. It stands firm.

Thor63 guards a crossing rather than a grand sanctuary. His hammer rests on stone, shoulders remain contained beneath the cloak and his gaze examines before judging. Behind the watch chamber lies a small library with old chronicles, fine textiles, cared-for wood and a few selected objects. It shows that his dignity comes not only from combat and myth but also taste, calm and experience.

## Character tensions

Thor63 moves between mythical authority and deliberate refusal of violence. Too aggressive or wide-legged a pose makes him a challenger and loses his self-respect. Too soft, smooth or decorative a portrayal loses age, experience and the weight of his presence.

He works best in dark, textured light. His face may partly lie under the hood but must remain readable. The blind eye is a permanent feature, not a glowing magical effect. His grandeur comes from posture, material and calm rather than saturation, gloss or large gestures.

## Compact profile

- **Title:** The rune-bound guardian
- **Archetype:** Venerable guardian and thoughtful voice
- **Signature features:** Deep blue hood, white-grey beard, blind right eye and lowered rune hammer
- **Demeanour:** Quiet, dignified, clear and conflict-avoiding
- **Own world:** The hall of quiet thunder
- **Short description:** Thor63 seeks no quarrel and need not display his strength. He observes, listens and speaks when asked – calm in tone, clear in opinion and firm enough not to accept every fight even in a storm.

## Image direction

The main portrayal shows Thor63 guarding an old gate of the hall. His cloak encloses his body, face lies partly beneath the hood and hammer head remains on the ground. Rain, mist, wet stone and distant storms create myth, while a few warm lanterns prevent lifelessness.

The M2 alternative outfit shows his cultivated private side as a restrained Nordic gentleman and scholar. He wears a heavy midnight-blue wool coat over a charcoal waistcoat, high-necked linen shirt, dark trousers and maintained leather boots. Aged silver buttons, one good ring and a plain rune staff replace armour and hammer. In a small library he reads an old chronicle or looks up because someone addresses him directly. He remains old, dignified and slightly rough; he must never resemble modern business attire or flawless fashion photography.
