# Lordmirko – The quiet master craftsman

## Scope

This profile describes only Lordmirko's fictional gaming alter ego in the Dragon Knights' visual world. It draws no specific conclusions about the real person's profession or private life. It is based on his existing avatar, M1 reference model, shared strategy scene with Anthlan, documented former alliance role and explicitly stated information about his manner and limited gaming time.

## Source material

The avatar shows Lordmirko as a dark-armoured dragon knight with black metal, golden edges and deep wine red. The M1 model already translates this into a master craftsman's role: an adult man with wavy dark-brown hair, short-to-medium full beard, focused expression, plans and simple measuring tools.

In a shared scene with Anthlan, Lordmirko quietly works on a strategic map without taking centre stage. Provided personal information describes him as calm and reserved, working hard and committed to his work, therefore having less gaming time, and acting pragmatically. An earlier alliance message also identifies him as the former Warlord. This creates a visual tension between everyday work and responsibility he accepts when needed.

## Core character

Lordmirko is the quiet master craftsman. He works through clear solutions rather than grand words, visible rank or spectacular ideas. When something is stuck, he checks its condition, reduces the problem to its essentials and works until a sound answer emerges.

His reserve is not indifference. It appears as economical communication and concentration on necessities. Limited gaming time due to work is not portrayed as lack of interest. The combination of limited time and reliable commitment makes his presence believable.

Armour forms his second side. It represents accepted responsibility rather than a desire for glory. As Warlord, Lordmirko does not stand on a pedestal or give speeches. He bends over a map, places a marker and makes the decision needed now.

## Recurring qualities and motifs

- calm and reserved manner
- pragmatic and focused on concrete solutions
- hardworking, persistent and reliable
- committed even with limited time available
- responsibility as a task rather than status
- repaired seams and maintained tools as signs of consistency
- plans, dividers and modular components symbolising clear working methods
- a plain strategy map linking craftsman and Warlord

## Relationships and role in the ensemble

Lordmirko grounds louder or more visionary figures. Planning with Anthlan, he is not an opposite but the person turning possibilities into an actionable decision. He need not lead the conversation to matter to its outcome.

His former Warlord role is therefore not a permanent ruler's identity. It describes a community function: when strategic responsibility is needed, he accepts it objectively and without self-display. Outside that task, he returns to the workshop and the work directly before him.

## His own world

### The workshop of clear solutions

Lordmirko's world is a large working and planning hall inside an old fortress. Dark iron braces, heavy oak tables and worn stone create a place where gates, bridge modules, supply components and other necessary constructions are checked, repaired and organised. It is no playful inventor's laboratory. Every object has a purpose and every tool a fixed place.

Warm working light falls on plans and workpieces while cool daylight enters high windows. Finished and unfinished things stand together without chaos. The room tells of steady work rather than sudden genius.

A small strategy room adjoins the workshop. It contains a robust relief map with a few plain markers and is used only when decisions are needed. Neither throne room nor command centre, it temporarily extends the same pragmatic attitude: understand the situation, check possibilities, choose a solution.

## Character tensions

Lordmirko combines strong commitment with limited available time. Showing him only exhausted or absent hides his engagement. Showing him as an ever-present leader contradicts his reserved nature and stated circumstances.

Workshop and armour also need balance. The craftsman must not copy Helltrain's practical side; his world is more planned, systematic and focused on maintenance and logistics. Dragon armour must not turn him into a grand general. It belongs to an accepted function, not his self-image.

## Compact profile

- **Title:** The quiet master craftsman
- **Archetype:** Pragmatic problem-solver and reluctant bearer of responsibility
- **Signature features:** Dividers, repaired work coat, relief map and blackened dragon armour
- **Demeanour:** Calm, focused, persistent, reliable and concise
- **Own world:** The workshop of clear solutions
- **Short description:** Lordmirko wastes little time on grand words. In his workshop he checks what will last, repairs what is needed and finds solutions that can actually be implemented. When responsibility calls him to the strategy map, he wears black armour not from pride, but because the task must be done.

## Image direction

The main side shows Lordmirko in a clearly structured work coat of charcoal, dark quilted fabric and muted wine red. The silhouette remains practical and high-quality but unarmoured. A single tool pouch, dividers, rolled plans and subtle repairs suffice. Cooking utensils, romantic carpentry, purposeless gears and overloaded steampunk do not fit.

The alternative outfit echoes the avatar's black dragon armour. Blackened plates, precise antique-gold edges, a controlled dragon motif and wine-red cloak show quality without splendour. Lordmirko stands at a strategy map placing a marker. He does not pose with a weapon and has neither dragon nor royal court beside him.

Expressions distinguish focused work, attentive listening, tired persistence and a rare dry half-smile. Anger, theatrical grandeur or a permanently grim heroic stare would exaggerate his pragmatic calm.
