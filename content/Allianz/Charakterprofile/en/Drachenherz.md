# Drachenherz – The dragon voice of the green shield

## Scope

This profile describes only Drachenherz's fictional gaming alter ego in the Dragon Knights' visual world. It draws no conclusions about the real person behind the player name.

## Source material

The basis consists of 35 identifiable avatars, character, chat and reaction images, plus additional information about the character's LARP-inspired origins. Recurring motifs include emerald dragon scales, quiet strength, shared planning, dry humour, practical help, closeness to nature and a strong protective instinct.

## Core character

Drachenherz is the dragon voice of the green shield: a steadfast protective cleric whose strength serves others above all. He does not push himself into the spotlight, but steps forward when someone needs protection, support or a reliable hand.

His calm is not coldness. Beneath the resilient armour is a warm-hearted, nature-loving soul who helps without seeking personal gain. He prefers acting together, carries responsibility without fuss and lets his actions speak.

## Recurring qualities

- He protects people, paths and shared endeavours rather than displaying strength for its own sake.
- He helps practically and reliably even without personal benefit.
- He speaks thoughtfully rather than loudly and observes carefully first.
- When action is needed, he often conveys a calm “I'll handle it.”
- His humour is dry, understated and occasionally self-deprecating.
- Nature, herbs and living plants reveal his gentle, caring core.
- Community grows through reliability, shared tasks and experiences.
- His strength is most effective as a shield for others.

## Relationships and role in the ensemble

Drachenherz is primarily a protector and reliable companion in our community. He does not need to be the loudest to carry weight. His influence grows where others know they can count on him.

He shares a quiet bond of trust with Anthlan. Maps, two cups and a calm place away from the bustle represent conversations where not everything needs explaining. Both share planning and responsibility without making a spectacle of it.

Within the group, Drachenherz grounds the livelier characters. He can join in jokes and avoid taking himself too seriously, but rarely loses sight of what is needed.

## His own world

### The grove of the dragon voice

Deep in the forest stands an old bastion whose walls are threaded with ivy, herbs and young trees. An abandoned fortification has become a protected resting place: paths lead safely to the fire, benches and equipment await companions, and medicinal herbs, ointments, bandages and seedlings are ready on a worktable.

Drachenherz protects this place as a guardian rather than a ruler. His emerald blessing spans the entire camp while he gives equal attention to supporting a damaged plant. The dragon-bone sword stays sheathed. It is present, but not the centre of his character – his real strength enables life, community and a safe return.

Two filled cups beside a shared map suggest familiar conversation with Anthlan without adding another figure. Empty, prepared places show that the grove was never meant for Drachenherz alone.

## Character tensions

Drachenherz combines the force of a holy warrior with a gardener's gentleness. Depicting him only as a heavily armed fighter loses his helpfulness, warmth and quiet attentiveness. Making him too gentle or harmless removes the steadfast strength that makes him a credible protector.

He works best as a quiet shield: physically strong, experienced in battle and ready to intervene, while attentive to small things, living nature and the people beside him.

## Image direction

Drachenherz belongs in an open woodland grove inside an overgrown bastion. The scene should resemble a credible camp for a travelling fantasy community, rather than a throne room or religious stage. Dark-green scale armour and the protected dragon-bone sword connect him with his martial origins; herbs, seedlings, travel gear and prepared seats show whom that strength serves.

A small caring action, such as supporting a damaged plant, forms the emotional centre. The emerald protective blessing can be large, but should cover the whole camp rather than elevate Drachenherz as a solitary chosen one. Warm woodland and firelight preserve his approachability.
