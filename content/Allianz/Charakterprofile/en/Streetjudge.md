# Streetjudge – The judge of the streets

## Scope

This profile describes only Streetjudge's fictional gaming alter ego in the Dragon Knights' visual world. It draws no conclusions about the real person's personal qualities. Statements about his nature refer to recurring portrayals in the existing images.

## Source material

The basis consists of three avatars, three chat images and the existing M1 reference model. Recurring motifs are a fantastical Berlin city world, the contrast between justice and chaos, direct humour, coffee, the dragon medallion and compass rose on his forearm. Cancer motifs in two reaction images add loyalty, sensitivity and inner strength to the fictional character.

## Core character

Streetjudge is not a judge above people, but one living among them. He knows the city's confusing routes, hears different sides and tries to find a fair, understandable line in chaotic situations. His authority comes from experience, directness and trust earned on the streets rather than rank, splendour or distance.

He remains approachable. A dry grin, cup of coffee and fitting remark belong to him as much as a serious look when a decision carries weight. He can be loud and clear without becoming domineering. At his core is the belief that community only works when nobody remains invisible.

## Recurring qualities and motifs

- clear and direct without unnecessary formality
- strong sense of fairness and understandable rules
- dry, occasionally loud humour
- loyal to the community
- attentive to easily overlooked people
- pragmatic amid chaos and unimpressed by mere status
- Berlin as a home motif and lively, contradictory city
- coffee as a grounded ritual, not a status symbol
- compass rose as a sign of orientation and different paths
- dragon medallion as visible alliance connection

## Relationships and role in the ensemble

Streetjudge brings clarity to situations shifting between joke, misunderstanding and real conflict. He does not belong in a distant throne room. His role resembles an experienced mediator who understands the street's language and expresses decisions that hold up beyond official rooms.

His humour makes him accessible without displacing his serious side. Where others work through strategy, diplomacy or caring calm, he asks directly: what is fair here, what is necessary and how do we move forward together?

## Visual identity

- middle-aged to older adult man with narrow, weathered face
- tousled medium-length brown hair and short, imperfect beard
- lean, mobile and street-experienced build
- dark charcoal layered clothing repaired several times
- burgundy scarf as recurring colour accent
- aged bronze dragon medallion
- compass-rose tattoo on the forearm
- leather, heavy wool, wet stone, brick and old brass
- smoky navy, charcoal, burgundy, brass and warm ivory
- fantastical Berlin skyline with bridges, arcades, roofs and a transformed television tower

## His own world

### The city of open paths

Streetjudge's Berlin lies among old brick architecture, high bridges, narrow alleys and weathered rooftop paths. The city is neither clean nor orderly, but alive. Warm window light falls onto wet paving while smoky-blue sky and the distant television tower offer orientation above the roofs.

His real workplace is not a courtroom but the transition between street and overview: a bridge, open arcade or roof from which he sees routes without distancing himself from people below. Here he listens, weighs things up and says what would otherwise be lost in noise and chaos.

## Character tensions

Streetjudge moves between approachable street figure and serious mediator. As merely a casual adventurer, he loses his name's justice core. As a stern judge or grand dignitary, he loses closeness to people.

His visual language therefore needs both the knowing half-grin of someone familiar with chaos and the calm direct gaze of someone accepting responsibility for a judgement at the decisive moment. Humour and sensitivity do not oppose clarity, but keep it from becoming cold.

## Compact profile

- **Title:** The judge of the streets
- **Archetype:** Experienced mediator between order and chaos
- **Signature features:** Burgundy scarf, compass rose, dragon medallion and Berlin rooftop paths
- **Demeanour:** Direct, fair, humorous, loyal and attentive
- **Own world:** The city of open paths
- **Short description:** Streetjudge knows the city's confusing routes and the people travelling them. He listens, speaks directly and seeks a fair line amid chaos – with dry humour, a coffee cup and the firm principle that community must leave nobody behind.

## Image direction

Streetjudge belongs in a fantastical, aged Berlin cityscape. His main side shows him in practical, repaired travel clothes on roofs, bridges or the street: alert, approachable and wearing a small knowing grin. City life remains palpable without crowds distracting from him.

The M2 alternative outfit shows his quiet, judging side. A clean-cut magistrate's coat of heavy charcoal wool with burgundy lining, weathered leather and aged brass replaces casual street clothes. A used city ledger represents listening and weighing decisions. Crown, gavel, modern police uniform or aristocratic splendour do not suit him.

Main and alternative sides must use different expressions: slightly ironic and open on the left, calm, serious and direct on the right. In both, Streetjudge remains part of the city and never stands above it.

## Established for the M2 model

- The fantastical Berlin city is his binding world.
- The main motif is the approachable street mediator, not a generic fantasy warrior.
- The alternative outfit shows a restrained magistrate and mediator.
- Burgundy scarf, compass rose and dragon medallion ensure recognisability.
- Coffee stays a small grounded detail rather than a dominant joke.
- The two large portrayals deliberately use different gaze directions and expressions.
