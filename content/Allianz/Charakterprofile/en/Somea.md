# Somea – The goddess of a thousand voices

## Scope

This profile describes only Somea's fictional gaming alter ego in the Dragon Knights' visual world. It draws no conclusions about the real player's personal qualities.

## Source material

The basis consists of 48 identifiable avatars, chat and reaction images, the character model and her depicted responsibility for community and membership management.

## Core character

Somea is our Goddess and the passionate heart of the alliance. Her particular strength is not standing above others, but making every person feel seen. She hears quiet voices too, notices anyone on the margins and addresses our members' worries and needs in her direct, personal way.

She does not protect our community from a safe distance. She engages with each person, listens, asks questions, gets involved and brings people back together. She combines genuine warmth with clarity and determination. Warmth and temperament are not opposites, but two sides of the same loyalty.

## Recurring qualities

- She treats every member as an individual person rather than merely a role, rank or game piece.
- She actively ensures quieter voices are heard and nobody remains on our community's margins.
- Community, belonging and personal recognition are central to her.
- She leads closely rather than distantly and openly shows enthusiasm, annoyance, pride and affection.
- She has strategic instinct and wants to shape decisions actively.
- She assumes authority quickly and soon develops her own agenda.
- She tests boundaries and often uses provocation as playful momentum.
- Her humour is theatrical, confident and occasionally deliberately exaggerated.
- Beneath the drama lie a soft heart and a strong need to protect the community.
- Patience is a strategy – though not always her preferred one.

## Relationships and role in the ensemble

Somea is Anthlan's emotional counterbalance and an important corrective voice. She brings feeling, intuition and determination into shared decisions while Anthlan more often tries to preserve order and groundedness. Drachenherz and mysteryZ create further playful friction revealing her directness, loyalty and possessiveness towards the coffee machine.

Within the alliance, she represents leadership's human side: worries, fairness, membership management and togetherness. She knows not just the community as a whole but sees its people individually. Her portrayal should therefore never be reduced to power, strategy or seduction.

## Visual identity

- midnight-blue to black hair with sapphire-blue highlights
- golden eyes
- black armour with gold and sapphires
- star, sky and storm motifs
- a large black companion cat with golden eyes
- maps, strategy tables and glowing connections
- fire and a red decision button as signs of her drive to act
- hearts, plush dragons and small doodles as a softer counterpoint
- Berlin and its television tower as geographical anchors
- coffee or the coffee machine as almost sacred property

## Her own world

### The salon of a thousand voices

High above a fantastical Berlin lies a nighttime salon where our alliance's voices, worries and wishes converge as golden and sapphire threads. Every place at the round table represents a member; every light represents a story. Somea knows their different signs, hears every voice and keeps connections alive through attention and care rather than controlling them like puppets. Anyone entering should immediately feel: you are seen, heard and belong with us. The black cat seeks her closeness trustingly; the red decision button stays in the background.

## Character tensions

Somea moves between warm Goddess, community guardian and confident troublemaker. Too much calm loses her unmistakable energy. Too much coldness, calculation or dominance removes her most important quality: noticing each person and giving them a sense of belonging. She works best when attentive, direct, influential and slightly dangerous to excessively boring plans at the same time.

## Compact profile

- **Title:** The goddess of a thousand voices
- **Archetype:** Attentive Goddess and heart of our community
- **Signature features:** Personal lights, connecting sapphire threads, black cat and Berlin nighttime salon
- **Demeanour:** Attentive, warm, direct and protective
- **Own world:** The salon of a thousand voices
- **Short description:** Somea sees the person behind rank, strength or role first. She listens, notices when someone grows quieter and ensures nobody stays on the margins. Through warmth, clarity and her unmistakable temperament, she turns many individuals into our shared home.

## Image direction

Somea belongs in a lively nighttime salon above Berlin, rather than a quiet star temple. The salon opens as a monumental loggia with immense arches, floating terraces and great vertical depth onto a fantastically magnified Berlin. Television tower, river and city lights remain recognisable but become her own world through slender towers, cloud bridges and dragon-like architecture. Sapphire-gold voice threads rise from the map table's individual lights into the sky, forming connections and constellations. A few strong light sources and dramatic night sky create more scale than clusters of uniform lanterns and small objects.

The round table, black cat and individual personal details still show that she notices everyone in our community. Her posture should be open and inviting, her gaze make real contact. A warm, attentive smile fits better than a calculating look or remote grandeur. Strategy remains visible but must never overshadow care and closeness.

The M2 alternative outfit shows her confident femme-fatale side in a floor-length evening gown of black velvet and midnight-blue silk. Sapphires, aged gold, star embroidery and transparent dark layers connect it with her world. Alone and thoughtful on a high balcony above nighttime Berlin, she gazes longingly into the distance. An open back, clear shoulder line, fitted silhouette and controlled leg slit convey adult sex appeal without becoming a pin-up pose, excessive exposure or modern red-carpet aesthetics. Coffee, decision button and cat do not belong in this alternative balcony scene.
