# Thank you for your efforts in Capital Conquest

Message about Capital Conquest on 10 October 2026, written on 11 October 2026. From Anthlan and our R4 officers to our members.

![Anthlan and our six R4 officers thank our members for their efforts.](../../../media/alliance/Hauptstadteroberung_Danke.png)

## Copy version for the alliance message

```html
<size=38><color=#FFD700><b>Thank you, everyone!</b></color></size>

Dear Dragon Knights,
I just want to say thank you – on behalf of our R4 officers too.

<b>Thanks to everyone who came to the capital with us: active and passive participants alike!</b> Whether you earned points or were there without fighting, I am happy you joined us. You are all part of our shared effort.

15 of us appear in the rankings with around 9.56 million points. That makes me happy – just as much as everyone whose presence is not visible there.

I know not everyone can always be there. It is lovely when we come together and everyone contributes what they can.

For now, it is time for a coffee and a heartfelt thank you from me.

<color=#FFD700><b>So glad you are here!</b></color>
Your Anthlan and your R4 officers
```

The German original's copy version contains 901 characters including formatting and line breaks and is below the 1,000-character limit. The English copy version is also below this limit.

## Basis

The figures come from the complete rankings, captured on 10 October 2026 at around 16:49. They describe the recorded score, rather than the outcome of the battle.

The image shows Anthlan, mysteryZ, Somea, DaVinci1986, Drachenherz, Helltrain and Thor63 in the regular semi-realistic fantasy style, based on all seven M2 model sheets. At the user's express request, the characters form part of the main subject for this personal thank-you message.
