# Our alliance rules

Our alliance rules protect us, prevent unnecessary conflict and keep our attacks and raids consistent with our server's agreements. When in doubt, check first and act afterwards. If you are unsure, please ask an R4 or R5.

## 1. Respect NAP10

We **do not attack members of the current NAP10 alliances**. Rankings can change, so we do not rely on an earlier position.

Before every attack, check the current rankings and your target's alliance again. If the alliance belongs to NAP10, or the situation is unclear, we do not attack. This helps prevent counterattacks and diplomatic conflicts that can affect us all.

## 2. Permitted base attacks

We may attack **players without an alliance** and players whose name follows the pattern **Commander\*\*\*\***. Even when a target is permitted, keep the attack proportionate: please do not reduce anyone completely to **0**.

A permitted attack does not give us free rein to keep attacking unnecessarily. End your attack once you have achieved your objective. Before any further attacks, check again whether the alliance name, rank or situation has changed.

## 3. Raid the right targets

We do not raid trucks, freight trains or special missions **on our own server**. Always select a target from **another server** in the relevant view.

Check the server number carefully before starting. An attractive reward or rare cargo does not override this rule. This protects cooperation on our server and prevents unnecessary disputes with other alliances.

## 4. If a NAP10 member attacks

If a member of a NAP10 alliance attacks you, our rule is: **do not retaliate**. A direct counterattack can complicate the resolution and turn a single incident into a larger conflict.

Instead, share the attack report and report the incident to an **R4 or R5**. Where possible, include the attacker's name, alliance and the time. Our alliance leadership will then coordinate the response and tell you what happens next.

## Rank and activity rules

### Rank 1 – Entry

New members have a **7-day probation period**. Commanders without a custom name and members with **7 or more days of unreported inactivity** also belong to rank 1. After 7 days of unreported inactivity, a **private message** is sent; without a reply, **removal from the alliance follows on day 9**.

### Rank 2 – Member

Regularly online and at the **alliance rally point**, but with little event participation. **Rank 2 can be permanent.**

### Rank 3 – Active

Regularly online and participating in **alliance events**. Not every event is mandatory; **overall participation** counts.

## Community and absences

Stay at the **alliance rally point**, answer important **private messages** and follow the rules. **Please report holidays, illness and breaks.** Ranks reflect activity and are **not a personal judgment**.

## Sanctions for rule violations

We follow this sanctions process as our shared approach to rule violations. We seek dialogue first; the maximum time for a reply and clarification is **24 hours**.

1. Report the incident to R4/R5: include a brief description, the players involved, alliance and time; attach the battle report for an attack.
2. Leadership coordinates contact with the player involved and their alliance leadership. Anyone contacted must reply within 24 hours and help clarify the incident.
3. Leadership coordinates the next steps. No independent retaliation and no automatic attacks.

| Violation | Dialogue within 24 hours | No dialogue within 24 hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st violation | Warning + removal of one special operation or transporter (of equal or higher value) | Base attack, without complete destruction |
| 2nd violation | Base attack, without complete destruction | Base attacks up to complete destruction |
| 3rd violation | Base attacks up to complete destruction | Not separately specified |

> Our principle: peaceful resolution first. The same standards apply to our own members. The table does not replace coordination with R4/R5.

## Membership in our alliance

Rule violations receive **2 warnings**. The next violation leads to **removal from the alliance**. Warnings remain valid **until they are lifted**.
