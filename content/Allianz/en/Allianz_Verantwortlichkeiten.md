# Our responsibilities

Our responsibilities give recurring tasks a clear home. They describe a person's area of responsibility within the alliance, rather than their rank. One person may take on several tasks; vacant roles are temporarily covered by alliance leadership.

## Leadership and community

### Butler and deputy – mysteryZ

mysteryZ stands in for Anthlan when needed, supports decisions affecting the whole alliance and helps with diplomatic agreements with other alliances.

### Goddess – Somea

Somea looks after our community and membership management. This includes organisational questions, concerns, fairness and how we get along together.

### Recruiter – DaVinci1986

DaVinci1986 supports prospective and new members from their first contact through to a successful start with us.

## Combat and events

### Warlord – Drachenherz

Drachenherz is responsible for PvP events, Pandora, rallies and overall combat coordination.

### Ambush coordinator – Helltrain

Helltrain plans and coordinates Alliance Ambush, gathers questions and makes sure all participants know the current plan.

## Development and supplies

### Train coordinator – DaVinci1986

DaVinci1986 organises the freight train, coordinates the arrangements and selects a suitable escort officer when needed.

### Research director – vacant, currently Anthlan

The role covering alliance research, research priorities and the leadership star is currently vacant. Anthlan handles this area until a suitable person is found.
