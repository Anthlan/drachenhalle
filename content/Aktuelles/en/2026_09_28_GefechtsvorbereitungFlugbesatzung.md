The limited-time **Aircrew Battle Preparation** event has begun. The opening sequence offers 15 Flying Star Coins per event day, or 75 over five days. Together with one free coin per shop reset, up to **83 free coins** are expected to be available.

The airport slot machine's daily point counter remains important: collect coins first, then spend them together to secure a daily milestone you can reliably reach. The counter resets daily.

Free-to-play players are best off saving their coins until the last safe day to use them and deciding how to spend supply vouchers after their draws. Targeted buyers can instead secure daily UR universal hero shards or individual deluxe chests. Choosing 5,000 refined stones from the chest gives twice the stone rate of the normal exchange.

The complete guide now compares both purchase routes using guaranteed values only. Based on the coins' guaranteed minimum reward, the skin would cost around €833; buying vouchers directly through a fully predictable route would cost around €596. A mixed strategy usually makes most sense: use free or very cheap coins one at a time, then purchase only the actual remaining voucher shortfall directly.

**Update of 4 October 2026:** New screenshots show all 15 reward levels. Requirements of 5, 5, 10, 10, 15 and 20 are confirmed for the first six. If your main aim with 280 coins is to maximise supply vouchers, two runs to level 9 are likely better than one deep run to level 15: all boxes visibly marked with vouchers appear at levels 1, 3, 5 and 7. Levels 13 to 15 each provide three UR survival chests containing a random UR survivor; without a choice, their value depends heavily on your account. Random symbols still mean your actual progress depends on the draws.

A practical test using 279 coins, mostly in 5× mode, produced only 1,500 new supply vouchers, or 5.38 per coin. Another 50 individual 1× draws yielded 460 vouchers, or 9.2 per coin. The theoretical average is the same in both modes; however, 1× spreads the risk across many more independent results and is therefore better suited to predictable voucher targets.

A subsequent direct purchase of 2,400 vouchers for €360 confirms the regular rate of €0.15 per voucher. The calculator therefore no longer counts theoretical rare wins as available purchasing budget.
