Our alliance has a new stage: **Alliance → Picture Stories** brings little adventures to life with our players as chibi characters. Our first story is **DIE and the Forbidden Button**, a three-page manga.

Anthlan has one clear request: do not press. Drachenherz, DaVinci1986 and Helltrain are curious, and even a safety cover, a lock and a chain cannot stop every good intention. Somea and mysteryZ complete the cast.

Enjoy generously sized comic pages, enlarged views and an additional text version. Images and text are available in German, English, French, Russian and Portuguese. This is a humorous fantasy tale about our in-game alter egos, not a game guide.

[Read the picture story now →](/drachenhalle/bildgeschichten/der-verbotene-knopf/)

## New picture stories on a regular basis

A special thank-you to **Somea**: She came up with the idea and did most of the work. Anthlan helped with a few parts. We want to share new adventures with you regularly.
