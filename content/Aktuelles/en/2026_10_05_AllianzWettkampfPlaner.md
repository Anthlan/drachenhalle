How many actions do you need for the next reward? Which Arms Race phase matches them? How many radar quests can we keep for the next scoring day?

Since the update of 6 October 2026, the [Alliance Duel points planner](/drachenhalle/en/tools/wettkampfplaner/) guides you through the five collection themes from Monday to Friday and Saturday's Enemy Attack. Enter the amounts of supplies you plan to use: the planner adds several actions using adjustable point values taken from our gameplay screenshots. Your plans are saved on your device for each weekday, provided your browser allows this.

The [knowledge article](/drachenhalle/en/docs/tipp-14-allianz-wettkampf-punkte-planen/) contains the weekly overview and complete points tables. Radar counts on Monday, Wednesday and Friday; the radar section calculates how much room you need before the next refill. Exact saving times and Arms Race windows still require confirmed game displays.
