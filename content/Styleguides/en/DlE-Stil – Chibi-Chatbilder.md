# DlE style – Chibi images

## Purpose

Chibi style S3 deliberately exaggerates and complements the cinematic and comic-like DlE visual language. It can be used for short reaction images as well as scenic or narrative chat images. Style alone does not determine an image's gallery folder.

## Suitable subjects

- exaggerated joy, anger, panic, embarrassment or disappointment
- friendly teasing and small mishaps
- recurring jokes about coffee, strategy, events or the attack button
- quick reactions and reusable alliance-chat greetings
- cute exaggerations of otherwise dramatic moments
- scenic or narrative chibi subjects with a clear small action

Serious alliance announcements and binding character references retain their designated formats. For chat and reaction images, deliberately choose S1, S2, S3 or S4 according to the subject.

## Appearance

- high-quality, polished 2D chibi fantasy illustration
- large heads, small bodies and clearly exaggerated expressions
- generally playful and often cute, but not childish or arbitrary; confirmed character-specific limits in profiles take precedence over this basic effect
- clear silhouettes and readable gestures
- simplified setting with a few selectively used details
- no mixing with photorealistic body proportions

## Characters

- For new chibi scenes, each figure's established reaction chibis define drawing style, face shape, eyes, proportions and expressions. Include them as image references. M1 and M2 models supplement references for character traits, clothing and setting; they do not replace existing chibi style references. This also applies to chibi illustrations in News and Analyses.
- existing character models remain binding references in chibi style
- clearly retain face, hairstyle, skin colour, horns, ears and other physical traits
- simplify distinctive clothing, colours, jewellery and accessories while keeping them recognisable
- chibi proportions may exaggerate the figure, but must not redesign it
- Character profiles also determine the degree of cuteness. Figures not wanting sweet or cuddly portrayals receive a more angular chibi interpretation with clearer silhouette, slimmer shapes and drier expressions.
- preferably one to three main figures per image

## Colours and DlE recognisability

- dark navy and gold remain the base colours
- warm golden and amber light connects the style to the wider DlE visual world
- use red or other strong colours selectively for the central joke or reaction
- simplified alliance halls, strategy tables, banners, maps and dragon ornaments as recurring settings

## Composition and humour

- prefer square format for short reaction images used in chat
- one clear joke or unambiguous reaction per image
- main figures and punchline must be immediately understandable even at small sizes
- use props sparingly and focus them on the punchline
- expression, posture and interaction primarily convey the joke without text

## Text in the image

- work without written text where possible
- if text is needed: no more than one short sentence or exclamation
- make text large, high-contrast and readable
- do not generate decorative pseudo-words or unnecessary labels

## Tone and effect

- friendly
- self-deprecating
- charming
- playful
- expressive
- never derogatory towards depicted players

## Avoid

- generic anime figures with no recognisable character-model connection
- strongly changing chibi drawing styles within a series
- overloaded backgrounds or competing punchlines
- oversexualised portrayal
- horror, gore or disturbing body distortions
- unreadable text, watermarks and additional unknown figures

## Storage and naming

Storage follows purpose:

- scenic or narrative subjects in `Galerie/Chatbilder`
- short reusable replies and greetings in `Galerie/Reaktionsbilder`

Both categories use the same naming pattern:

```text
YYYY_MM_DD_HHMM_ShortTitle_[MainFigures].extension
```

Examples:

```text
2026_09_23_0751_AngriffsknopfChibi_Somea_Anthlan.png
2026_09_25_2331_Passt_Anthlan.png
```

`AngriffsknopfChibi` is an S3 chat image because of its scenic action. `Passt` is an S3 reaction image because of its reusable purpose. Reaction images may also be assigned S1 or S2. Binding style assignments are in `media/gallery/STILINDEX.md`.
