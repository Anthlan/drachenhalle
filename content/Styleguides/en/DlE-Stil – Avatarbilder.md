# DlE style – Avatar images

## Purpose

Avatar images should make a player instantly recognisable and work at small in-game sizes. The character model remains more important than decorative details.

## Format and composition

- Square format (1:1), preferably high-resolution.
- Show head and upper body large and clearly recognisable.
- Do not place the face too close to the edge; leave room all around for the game interface frame.
- Gaze direction and pose may vary, but the face must remain recognisable.
- Consistently retain important character-model features.
- Do not place important details in the outermost corners.

## Style

- High-quality dark fantasy with realistic to slightly stylised character design.
- Strong contrast, cinematic light and a clear focus on the figure.
- Base palette: black, dark red, metal and warm light accents.
- Use dragon motifs, banners, chains or castles only as supporting elements.
- Detailed background, but calmer and less dominant than the figure.

## Lettering

- Player name large and readable in the lower image area.
- Lettering may overlap chest or armour, but must not cover the face or central features.
- Metallic medieval typography matching DlE style.
- Leave enough distance from the bottom edge so the name is not cropped in the game.

## Quality control

- Also check the image at small avatar size.
- Name, face and key identifying features must be instantly readable.
- No unintended extra limbs, weapons or distorted lettering.
- Name the final file clearly after the player, for example `Anthlan.png`.
