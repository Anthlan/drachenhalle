## DlE style – Alliance announcements

**Goal:**  
Clear, reusable information graphics for alliance announcements. The content should be understandable at a glance and work at small in-game sizes.

### Audience and perspective
- Alliance announcements primarily address our own members.
- Write accompanying articles, graphic text and copy versions from our shared perspective: preferably with **we**, **us** and **our**, addressing members directly with **you** and **you all**.
- Explain our rules, responsibilities and processes as part of the community. Avoid outside wording such as “the alliance” or “its members” where “we” or “our members” fits naturally.
- Factual names and general game mechanics may remain neutral; frame recommendations and organisational statements as our shared approach.
- The complete overarching rules are in [`DlE – Text formatting`](DlE%20%E2%80%93%20Textformatierung.md).

### Appearance
- Elegant **blue-and-gold Dragon Knight look**
- Warm fantasy background with:
  - castle hall
  - banners
  - candles
  - map or strategy table
  - books, parchments and small dragon details
- No figures as the main subject
- High-quality, clean, structured and more **informative than narrative**
- Golden ornaments and frames, dark navy as the base colour
- Warnings may use **red** for selective highlighting

### Structure
- Large, highly readable heading
- 2–4 clearly separated information blocks
- Symbols for quick orientation, for example:
  - shield
  - clock
  - calendar
  - attack
  - shop
- Little text, large and unambiguous
- Always highlight important times and actions
- German local time + server time where relevant
- Content must be understandable without accompanying text

### Tone and effect
- Factual, confident and friendly
- No excessive drama
- A light alliance atmosphere rather than a plain infographic
- Intended feeling:
  **“An official DlE announcement from our strategy room.”**

### Avoid
- Too many figures
- Overloaded text
- Apocalyptic red-and-black appearance
- Modern science-fiction UI
- Busy composition
- Small, hard-to-read lettering
